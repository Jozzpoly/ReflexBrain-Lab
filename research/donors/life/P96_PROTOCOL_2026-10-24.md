This is an undeclared *perfect localization scaffold* by strict ReflexBrain actor-private standards. Do NOT treat the later body-relative output as proof of a fully privately earned position estimate; a genuine future transfer needs odometry/error and audio source-location falsification.
2. **Underspecified positive tests**: upstream `prediction.test.ts` 'same genome different lives' asserts differences **only if at least one matching pair exists** (`if(compared>0)`); 'experienced less surprised' asserts when `greenN>4&&seasonedN>4`. Green tests can be non-informative with zero matching pairs. Mechanism unit tests are real but do not prove ordinary-life causality.
3. Original LIFE code **has no actor-changed persistent solid/material topology or object carrying yet**; developer names it under 'unbuilt'. Bodies are genetic ellipses, not morphogenetic multicell structures. This donor is most promising for private lifetime cognitive plasticity, not immediate Rapier/ALIEN embodied topology.
