# 1. Current provisional causal substrate

## Ecology: D0 dynamic mechanical micro-world

One focal actor in a small but causally rich physical world.

World should include:
- persistent material state;
- partial occlusion;
- at least one independent lawful non-agent process;
- at least one material relation that can alter topology or future access;
- multiple possible local continuations;
- delayed/persistent consequences;
- more than one timescale.

No second actor initially.

Peer insertion remains a later controlled comparison.

## Body

Preserve E0 force/torque/controller seam as donor.

Do not assume disc morphology is final.

Design body boundary so:
- B0 disc can run unchanged;
- B1 minimal asymmetric rigid body can be swapped under the same controller interface.

Morphology comparison is V1, not prerequisite to initial causal world research.

## Perception: P1 candidate

Base sensory substrate:
- proprioception/contact;
- body-relative local geometry;
- occlusion;
- simple sensory appearance/motion evidence.

Add a replaceable temporary perceptual tracklet scaffold:
- actor-private temporary token;
- no World ID;
- no semantic object role;
- only evidence-supported continuity.

Long-term identity remains memory hypothesis.

## Memory

Minimal candidate:
- recent sensorimotor history;
- current/recent track continuity;
- sparse private relational hypotheses;
- provenance/timestamps;
- checked-absence capability as principle;
- self/body predictive parameters if/when learned.

No global authoritative scene graph.

## Action organization: C-A baseline

A persistent local closed-loop controller/process:
- consumes private evidence;
- has its own temporal causal state;
- emits local motor/interaction demand;
- tracks contradiction/progress evidence;
- can recover/terminate.

No hidden routes/coordinates.

No label is accepted as evidence of continuation.

## Self/world axis

Preserve:
- motor demand;
- actuator/body response;
- proprioception/contact;
- sensory consequence.

Provide research hooks for:
- analytic or learned forward prediction;
- residual comparison.

Behavior need not use residual initially.

## Owner

Initial modes:
- observer;
- legal world manipulation;
- body perturbation.

Temporary full body takeover is no longer required in specimen 0.

Reason:
it is a strong intervention and may complicate ownership/attribution before ordinary organism continuity is established.

## Microscope

Minimal:
- World truth;
- private sensory evidence;
- private state/history;
- active local controller state;
- motor demand;
- physical outcome;
- exogenous interventions;
- first divergence for paired runs.

Do not build broad oracle/corpus infrastructure before a phenomenon earns it.

# 2. The unresolved slot — what sustains the actor's trajectory?

Do not bake one answer into substrate.

The same substrate should support small research specimens representing distinct hypotheses:

## T0 — authored grounded concern control

Purpose:
establish maximum interpretability and private-epistemic competence.

Claim allowed:
an embodied private agent can maintain an authored concern.

Not:
endogenous normativity.

## T1 — developmental competence specimen

Purpose:
test whether learning-progress/controllability over sensorimotor contingencies creates useful self-directed trajectory without noisy-TV/representation pathologies.

## T2 — adaptive habit specimen

Purpose:
test whether a self-maintaining sensorimotor organization creates continuity and disruption-relative significance without collapsing to a hidden cycle.

Potential later:

## T3 — capability-state modifier

Add body capability/load state to T0/T1/T2 as a source of real stakes, not sole motivation.

---

# 3. Why one shared substrate may be scientifically justified

The substrate choices above concern:
- lawful body/world coupling;
- private sensory boundary;
- private history;
- independent world causality.

They do not by themselves specify:
- what the actor cares about;
- what it learns;
- what semantic meaning is.

If the same world/body/perception can host T0/T1/T2, then comparison isolates trajectory/normativity mechanisms better than separate custom worlds.

---

# 4. Danger — research framework creep

This can become another R3-style apparatus.

Hard bounds:

- do not implement a generic plugin architecture;
- do not design universal interfaces for all future organisms;
- only support the minimum variants actually tested;
- one visual organism surface;
- one world;
- one body boundary;
- one perception/history implementation;
- small local differences between T0/T1/T2.

If supporting variants requires major abstraction work, choose one specimen instead.

# 5. First potential semantic pressures

If this substrate works, naturally occurring later questions may include:

- unexplained sensory/body change;
- stale private expectation;
- ambiguous relevance of dynamic process to ongoing trajectory;
- whether to persist, check, or redirect after partial contradiction;
- whether a novel percept resembles a consequential prior interaction;
- whether current effectivity has changed.

These are not yet ReflexBrain outputs.

They are candidate residuals to observe.

# 6. Current strongest reason this candidate may still be wrong

It may be **too researcher-neutral**.

By postponing normativity and trying to support multiple trajectory mechanisms, we could build a beautiful empty platform with no organism.

This is exactly microscope/framework gravity in a new form.

Therefore:

> no substrate implementation should begin until we can describe the first actual T0/T1/T2 specimen that will inhabit it and what phenomenon that specimen is expected to expose.

The substrate is only justified through a concrete organism specimen.
