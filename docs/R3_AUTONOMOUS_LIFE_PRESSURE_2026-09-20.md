# R3 Autonomous Life Pressure — 2026-09-20

Status: **ACTIVE IMPLEMENTATION · NO OWNER TEST · NO LEARNED BRAIN PROMOTED**

## Why R2 failed

The R2 live intervention specimen was rejected by the Owner because it was not life.

It was a causal research clicker:
- World advanced primarily because the Owner pressed buttons;
- activity was scripted rather than self-sustaining;
- the UI was richer than the organism;
- research controls manufactured the appearance of agency;
- there was no independent multi-actor causal ecology.

That branch remains evidence only.

## Corrected decomposition

ReflexBrain needs three distinct things:

1. **host organism** — world + body + private perception + persistent autonomous activity;
2. **local live brain** — cheap continuous competence that keeps the actor functioning without LLM ownership;
3. **ReflexBrain candidate** — learned semantic/reflex capability that earns a role inside an already-living actor.

Do not use layer 3 to fake layers 1–2.

## R3 immediate objective

Build a headless autonomous world that creates real cognitive pressure with **zero Owner input**.

The first world is intentionally small but must satisfy stronger invariants than the R2 clicker:

- three independent residents;
- fixed-step world time;
- material objects with World-owned factual state;
- private sight rather than global state access;
- private object memory with checked absence;
- persistent resident activities;
- World-authoritative move/pickup/place/process outcomes;
- causal dependence between residents;
- no scripted `tick === N` story beats;
- no buttons, Owner intervention hooks, or Owner-facing UI;
- life continues when nobody is observing it.

## Bounded research ecology

The initial ecology is a material work chain:

- **Mira / steward** tries to keep the workshop input rack supplied from a remote source;
- **Janek / worker** consumes a visible raw blank, processes it at a workbench and leaves a finished part at output;
- **Ida / courier** collects visible finished parts and carries them to a depot.

These role policies are **disposable fixture policies**, not the target architecture.

The reusable research assets are narrower:
- autonomous fixed-step host;
- private perception boundary;
- World action authority;
- persistent activity identity;
- material causal consequences;
- cross-actor interference/dependence;
- trace/evidence suitable for later ReflexBrain insertion.

## Promotion gates

R3 does not become a living host merely because tests are green.

Minimum deterministic evidence:

1. zero-input run creates repeated material cycles;
2. all enabled residents act without Owner commands;
3. removing the steward materially changes downstream worker/courier outcomes;
4. resident policy receives private observation only, never a World snapshot;
5. distant material state is absent from private observation;
6. revisiting an empty last-known location can invalidate stale object belief;
7. activity identity persists across many world ticks while physical execution progresses;
8. material production/delivery can be reconstructed from World events.

Only after this host survives red-team should ReflexBrain candidates be inserted.
