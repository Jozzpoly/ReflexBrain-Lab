## Why it changes the prior research steering
1. **Substrate-versus-controller confound partly separated.** With unmodified Rapier bodies and zero independent mechanical sources, the seeded random policy naturally re-encounters earlier disturbed material in both layouts. Merely adding lots of new physics is NOT logically required for a basic second encounter. Nevertheless, no proven *actor-private recognition* or acquired competence appears.
2. **Learned motor proxy is strongly vulnerable to action attractors.** Adaptive A chose commands `[72,3,3,2]` (79 completed scored trials), adaptive B `[77,1,1,1]`, overwhelmingly the same forward maneuver. A moved ordinary matter cumulatively 14.76475 world-units (1-second sampling) in first 30s, then **0,0,0** later; the actor continued 89+ units/30s. This is not a learning advantage but a durable mismatch of optimized bodily mobility with relational opportunities.
3. **Frozen control also failed to re-encounter**, despite balanced chosen actions `[20,20,20,20]`; balanced action counts are not automatically beneficial either.
4. **Authored Occupant's A contacts elsewhere continued to t90**, but never with the early-altered part. RGB occupancy remained nonzero in A throughout, yet no actor-private material meaning was learned.
5. **Mirrored B is a critical qualifier:** most non-random arms had **zero early altered parts**, making the specific re-contact conditional scientifically INCONCLUSIVE. Do not reclassify these zeroes as evidence of failure to re-meet altered matter.

## Interpretation and next action (not retrospective tuning)
X1 rejects a universal "no late contact possible on existing R8" reading and strongly cautions against promising a semantic learner from mobility-score updates. It cannot establish statistical superiority of randomized movement: there is only ONE random seed and two manually specified layouts. **Next pre-register a separate small seed-sensitivity control on unchanged random motor law**, to measure whether its two successes persist beyond the single seed; do not change material positions, optimize seed, retune controller or alter this result. Then evaluate a single real *actor-private consequence consumer* if the host naturally provides encounters. Conversely, if rare returns are unstable, first consider constraints/repertoire before building another learner.
