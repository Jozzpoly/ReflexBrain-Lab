## Where agency is authored rather than endogenous (source audit)
- [2025 Science Advances official article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12577699/), [MIT code](https://github.com/flowersteam/sensorimotor-lenia-search): IMGEP diversity search + differentiable optimization + progressive perturbation curriculum *find* robust moving structures. Source paper defines stable/moving/robust filters and a basic obstacle challenge of **50 × 2000-step rollouts** with externally arranged obstacles. This is a legitimate robust sensorimotor result, but designer-chosen search goals and population selection are NOT personal motives of a discovered structure.
- Author paper explicitly says the demonstration of attraction to a **cyan-like marker** used an **additional local rule** found by separate random search, filtering and human assessment; not originally a naturally evolved preference/need. It cannot qualify ReflexBrain's long-sought intrinsic meaning or refute Owner's criticism of ball chasing.
- [Original Flow-Lenia parameter-embedding implementation](https://github.com/erwanplantec/FlowLenia/blob/main/flowlenia/flowlenia_params.py): cell-wise parameter field `P` participates in material transport/mixing, opening a potential substrate for local inheritance of *rules*, not yet a child's or adult's lived learning. The optional example `beam_mutation` explicitly applies externally generated spatial gene perturbations; do not silently attribute mutation source to a self-maintaining individual.
- [Original reintegration operator's hidden-parameter branch](https://github.com/erwanplantec/FlowLenia/blob/main/flowlenia/reintegration_tracking.py): default stochastic mixing instantiates fixed `jax.random.PRNGKey(42)` inside that method. Repeated calls need not represent an advancing independent random stream. Do not claim a qualified autonomous mutation engine from default RNG code. **Not** exercised or reproduced in O2.

These findings triangulate **relational organization**, not artificial consciousness, autonomy or individual history. They span different hypotheses and **cannot be converted into cross-substrate comparative rankings**.

Avoid a repeated one-mechanism mini-gadget series R0–R5. Next run should move from conservation/robustness of state to **causal persistence of one discernible individual under mutual interaction with independently acting world processes**, with a real biographical intervention, and transparent researcher-vs-agent control. A good null is a physically equivalent externally moved field that *looks alive* but has no own historical dependence. Assess practical reproduction of a single already-trained paper pattern or mass/parameter embedding in a materially living situation *before* any elaborate new hybrid engine. No PR merge, deployment, Box2D, Jaskinia modifications or Owner product PASS.
