## Motivation and comparison
R10/R11 show anonymous scalar Lenia patterns can interact and leave two coherent fields, but **source information cannot yet establish continuous cell ancestry or actor-owned agency through a merge**. Flow-Lenia authors have dynamic local P but our FL0/FL1 used researcher-authored seed material. OpenPraparat is a conceptually *different* donor: multi-cell 3D bodies with actual energy and resource accounting, cell-local book/marker instructions, division and springs, in a shared world with interactions and no target fitness. [Peer-reviewed source paper, Utimula, *Artificial Life* 31(1), 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00466). These strong high-level ideas remain **external published evidence**, not qualified in our runtime. Authors report computing 10^8 steps and 2–3 months to discover example types, so **never** attempt their original whole evolution campaign as today's task.
