## Why this and not another corridor
R7/P0 source CI #39805607201 showed physically displaced material can persist and yet the source body itself obstructs the same future passage. One object displacement metric misses the entire environment configuration. Repeating authored rail/gate puzzles or refining a motor policy would be the old failure. Splot Next's source-confirmed part lifecycle (https://github.com/Jozzpoly/Splot/blob/research/material-part-lifecycle-2026-10-20/docs/research/2026-10-20-material-part-lifecycle.md) makes authored 3D solid parts physically anchored/free; donor IDEA ONLY, not a Box3D engine migration.

**New research tool, not new organism:** one open unbounded Rapier2D world with a continuing physical body, legal private 96RGB/touch/proprio and several independent rectangular material parts. Human can add, reposition, anchor/release and remove parts at runtime, manipulate world while paused or alive, then hand motor back to a cheap existing authored occupant. Multiple configurations and physical consequences are possible, not a built-in "open door" task. All part creation/mode/movement is EXPLICIT OWNER intervention, NEVER an intrinsic need or personal skill of actor.

## Frozen testable contract
1. Stable physical collider identity for material parts across FIXED<->DYNAMIC changes. Actual Rapier body type and contact dynamics change. No drawing-only fake or reconstructed body; no IDs/mode leak to the private frame.
2. Same initial body/motor demand tape and same part pose: FIXED part resists ordinary body push, DYNAMIC part moves under physically solved contact; real object position distinguishes regimes and private rays/touch respond through unchanged sensors.
3. Exact World checkpoint (including mode, part ID, pose, object list) and identical restored replay for at least 300 solver updates, with true tick counting. Dynamic→anchored→dynamic and deleting one part must not corrupt other objects.
4. World is open; no perimeter. Actor autonomy and human manual override are allowed but DO NOT entail learning/biological life.
