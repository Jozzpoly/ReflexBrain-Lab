## Why this exact experiment
Test physical *historical hysteresis*, not repeated machine phase and not a claim of actor memory. A real bounded-force actor pushes an ordinary object, stops acting, and leaves its real Rapier body in the world. The object remains at a physically acquired position rather than reverting to a scene-spawn or host quest reset. An independent matched no-action arm controls for world drift. A **fresh same-body future witness** is then put into each resulting world configuration by an explicitly host-owned reconstruction; this proves/disproves a future *world affordance difference*, not continuous individual organism biography.

## Locked R2a apparatus
- Two physically identical zero-gravity B0 local open lane scenes, no perimeter, two simple side walls, one dynamic ordinary disk initially centered at x=3.5,y=0, r=.75. Same Rapier 120Hz and legal RGB/touch/proprio 30Hz.
- DRIVE history: for **480 ticks** motor `{drive:.55,turn:0,gazeRate:0}`; afterwards **3600 ticks** motor zero. NULL history: same duration, zero motor always. No ghost moves while creating the lived history; no ID/position leaked to agent.
- Audit displacement of ordinary disk at 480/4080 vs its initial position; if both near zero, hypothesis FAIL. Require direct material trace >0.5 world units for action history, <0.05 for matched zero-action history, **and** trace after 30s idle stays >0.5 away. Values are research detection thresholds, not reward or learned skill.
- Exact full Rapier + private frame checkpoint at 480 ticks, simulate 3600 zero-motor, restore and repeat, compare full physics snapshot bytes, surface positions and 30Hz frame. No lossless inter-process claim.
- Future witness **separate explicit counterfactual**: new same B0 world/actor starts at origin; host creates identical generic object at *the physically measured, settled* location from DRIVE vs original location from NULL, with zero initial velocity. Run fixed forward motor .55 for 600 ticks in both, and host-audit first ordinary-object contact tick plus initial legal retina. This is not continuation of same actor; this is a controlled world-history transplant (velocities and previous surrounding material processes excluded). If source-generated location does not change first contacts/private retina, R2a's claimed future affordance is negative.
- All source-derived object/actor identifiers and positions remain researcher-only. Forward motor is deliberately blind; proving different encounters is **not** evidence that an agent benefits from memory, predicts events or cares about the object.

## Bounded interpretation / stop rule
Narrow candidate PASS only if action history produces durable measured material displacement under identical zero-motor wait and the fresh witness's lawful percept/contact opportunity differs from NULL. Even then: *engineered persistent material trace*, not independent ecology, learned actor meaning, autobiographical competence, ongoing activity, or artificial life. If any hard gate fails, record as negative; don't move the object, change initial radius, motor or thresholds after inspecting results. A new proposal is R2b and must be separately armed.
