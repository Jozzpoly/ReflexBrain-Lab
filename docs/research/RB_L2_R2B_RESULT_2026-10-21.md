### Causal interpretation / limitation
- **World position persistence alone is insufficient for ongoing actor-relative significance.** An actor moving a disk forward can simply carry its own encounter frame along with that disk; larger absolute displacement appears dramatic in host logs but hardly changes future local contact.
- Co-motion does **not** imply every real environmental consequence is negligible: disk moved beyond prior lane geometry, which could matter if there were independent activities, routes or fixed material anchors. This specimen did not show such a consumer.
- Blind scripted backtrack and return **already suffice** to physically re-encounter. R2b says nothing about autonomy, desire to return, useful memory, internal prediction, or late adaptation.
- Alternative reconstruction in R2a and same-body continuation in R2b ask different legitimate questions; do not collapse or declare one method universally superior.
- 30s pause is *simulated physical time with motor zero*, not a stopped OS process. No L2 long-lived multi-process continuation yet.
