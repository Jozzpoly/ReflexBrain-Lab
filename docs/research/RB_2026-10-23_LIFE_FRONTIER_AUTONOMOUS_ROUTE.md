## Mechanistic implication — high priority but still hypothesis
Our learner's chosen *personal mobility* objective can reward leaving a complex material environment forever: in X1 A it produced 14.76 units of 1s-sampled early material travel followed by 0 in each later 30s bin, while the body continued ~89 units / 30s. This is a **feedback starvation** risk. Its learned representation is over the reward for moving itself, not over causally distinct opportunities with material. Merely increasing training time strengthens an action attractor. Conversely, pure stochastic maneuver changes may maintain *access* without acquiring *meaning*. Neither is a complete living organism.
