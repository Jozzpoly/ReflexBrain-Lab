## Mechanistic implication — high priority but still hypothesis
Our learner's chosen *personal mobility* objective can reward leaving a complex material environment forever: in X1 A it produced 14.76 units of 1s-sampled early material travel followed by 0 in each later 30s bin, while the body continued ~89 units / 30s. This is a **feedback starvation** risk. Its learned representation is over the reward for moving itself, not over causally distinct opportunities with material. Merely increasing training time strengthens an action attractor. Conversely, pure stochastic maneuver changes may maintain *access* without acquiring *meaning*. Neither is a complete living organism.

## CANDIDATE SOURCE AUDIT — one opening mechanism, still a hypothesis, not a committed architecture
After X2, **do not attempt to manufacture repeat visitation** with better pathfinding, proximity penalties, toy circular walls or a stochastic timer. One promising alternative missing from all currently tested integrated organisms is **history-dependent body↔material structure**: material contact can alter the body's effective shape/mass/physical opportunities over time (e.g. reversible attachment/loss of a loose piece) and may alter the world's other accessible surfaces. This is not an automatically chosen feature and must compete against simpler material-state changes.

Why the candidate deserves source-level feasibility research:
- R8's `LivingWorld` source has one pre-given rigid B0/B1 body and rectangular parts with locked rotation. `Demand={drive,turn,gazeRate}`; material push changes ordinary positions but **never** actor's body or repertoire. Additional long training cannot produce action affordances absent from physics.
- Splot's `box3d-live.mjs` has actual Box3D gravity, dynamic crate and impulse-grip controlled by the human `w.grip`, with bounded reciprocal reaction; the independent boar `boar-intent.mjs` is **only** authored `travel/feeding/wary`. There is no evidence boar can autonomously attach/build or gain a new capacity. Splot can donate contact mechanics and support, **not organic motivation or a complete engine architecture**.
- Rapier's current [JavaScript joint docs](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/joints/) describe dynamic fixed/revolute joints, with explicit distinction between multi-collider and impulse-joint bodies. This is a **public feasibility hint only**, NOT a tested compatibility guarantee for our pinned `@dimforge/rapier2d-deterministic-compat@0.20.0`.
- Gloop's source `v1-mirror.mjs` ties genome scalars to the one forage/metabolize/reproduce loop, giving a clear warning against replacing real bodily affordances with more input parameters.
- R6 bottom-up fields offer self-maintaining organization as another possible substrate; previous false `t500` and non-survival forbid advertising it as organism already.

**Lowest-risk source next run (R9 feasibility, not performance trophy):** compare *one reversible contact-bond/material incorporation law* against the unbonded identical physical null and a simpler persistent world-only material modification. A bond must form/break through local material events under physically declared forces, not an actor command `build(goal)` or secretly assigned ownership; body shape/drag/mass and legal proprio/touch must respond. Record actual causal difference in later available maneuvers/opportunities in multiple spatial states, including negative/awkward consequences (sticking in passage, changed steering, dropping piece), not just number of joints or more collisions.

**Abandon early** if: all effects reduce to cosmetic cargo or authored quest steps; a single trivial material pose shows no new later physical options; numerical instability or forced body resets consume the run; or the only apparent "life" comes from a special controller driving towards the bonding prop. In that case audit other generative alternatives (local deformable physical trace, ecological inheritance/producer-consumer feedback, R6 self-organization) without preemptively committing to one physics engine.

**Only after a physical law survives:** run a bounded autonomous actor with private observation, historical modification and cheap reactive and stochastic controls, then ask if a learned actor-private distinction changes meaningful subsequent conduct. There is no commitment to consciousness, metabolism, artificial desires, neural architecture or a single universal definition of life.


## ACTUAL R9 evidence after X1/X2 — 23 October, qualifies feasibility not life
A separate [R9 research-only draft #91](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/ReflexBrain-Lab/pull/91) was created directly from untouched R8 owner-test code. Its experiments are real on pinned Rapier physics and do NOT modify R8 source or deploy to Owner.

- [R9 verified physics](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/ReflexBrain-Lab/actions/runs/39878430172): after direct collider impulse at t38, the material physically forms a fixed 2D joint to B0. In a narrow gap an actual material-wall impulse breaks it at t195; in a wider gap the joint endures 2400 real solver steps. No part / free part / fixed world part physically behave differently. A false TS-test callback and false float-timestep equality caused prior engineering RED; preserved and corrected by counting actual steps.
- [R9B mass-only counterfactual](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/ReflexBrain-Lab/actions/runs/39879027840): actor/cargo positions and velocities genuinely identical until physical contact t38, then a deliberately UNPHYSICAL mass-only replacement (remove cargo collider, add 2.1 to actor mass) produces distinctly different trajectories from a real protruding joint in both widths. This is not a clean isolated proof of `shape only`: inertia distribution, constraint forces and friction also differ. Not a possible organism life history.
- [R9C lawful private senses](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/ReflexBrain-Lab/actions/runs/39879623536): dual original `LivingWorld` 120Hz simulations with test-only unlocked part rotation, same motor and identical nine initial 30Hz private frames; true contact plus bond at t38 first changes actor-private retina/touch/proprio at legal t40. True joint relative geometry remains stable. Crucially, it demonstrates **private sensory access**, NOT learned significance or action adaptation.

**Revised source-grounded assessment:** one generative material law can indeed modify a body's physically possible behavior and produce lawful, temporally localized experience without changing the nominal 3-channel motor grammar or injecting World metadata. This rescues the *substrate feasibility hypothesis*, not the larger life thesis. It does NOT show a naturally self-assembling organism: attachment/break thresholds are authored, and steering remains an identical externally prescribed tape. Overfitting to box-joints as our new universal ontology would repeat the old gated-puzzle mistake.

**Next research frontier:** before expanding the substrate or rewarding a new learner, discover a **continuing physical/material process that offers the same individual both an opportunity and a cost under naturally achieved personal histories**. Need repeated opportunities without an imposed `return_to_box` motor, and plausible embodied practices distinct from baseline movement. Compare R9's local bond against broader material or ecological alternatives and Splot/real-world tactile feedback. If no such repeatable consequential involvement emerges, do not promote joints to organism architecture; retain them as a donor. Once a physical personal-history dilemma exists, run the cheapest private history consumer against naive reactive/random controls, keeping host truth separate from private evidence.
