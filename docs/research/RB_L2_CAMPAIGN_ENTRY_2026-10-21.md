## 4. Falsifiers, risk controls, stop/go
- **Ecology-only motion**: process dynamics/colored geometry change in host but the actor's available options and lawful history never change. No 'World PASS = ecology PASS'.
- **Puppet continuation**: actor appears to have a concern because test script provides every action opportunity or resets/relocates matter under its nose. Authored concern/activity must be identified and adversarially tested.
- **Metric laundering**: elapsed time, path distance, contact counts, ray hits, reward, learner steps or data volume passed off as learning to live. Owner FAIL outweighs machine engineering PASS.
- **No-contact blindness**: legal retina exists but chosen resident ignores it, or floor mechanisms are shown only to debug camera. No reason to train high-level meaning model.
- **Overfitting**: no iterative rescue of hand-picked route and target after frozen comparative failure; new hypothesis/run if necessary.
- **Pseudo-biography**: two trajectories diverge only because worlds or RNG differ; the actor's private retained history is not shown to be a causal variable.
- **Fidelity/identity falsehood**: checkpoint does not restore all World process states; frozen RGB not distinguished from post-intervention state; owner takeover credited as self-chosen training. Hard execution FAIL.
- **Run inflation**: don't open 5 PRs before one meaningful result, or run two-hour CI per docs-only push. Leave old #84 unchanged; branch new L2 from its exact qualified head and commit related experiment + evidence in small completed increments. Do not merge or publish unqualified work.

**Decision discipline:** failure in an honest new specimen may be the highest-value result. If no bounded physical practice can survive R0/R1, reconsider why we are making a life-shaped controller before building more mechanics. If a valuable H7/H8 event is found, progress ReflexBrain proper even while world quality remains imperfect.
