## New falsifiers with meaningful scientific pressure
Do not ask only whether the new thing moves, keeps mass, makes obstacles avoidable or survives a small wound.
1. **Identity:** is there a locally maintainable, causally stable *individual boundary* which can form/change/disappear under the same law that governs surroundings? A named fixed Rapier actor is a distinct starting assumption, not a discovery.
2. **Autonomy/provenance:** what material flows/decision signals are generated *inside* that individual rather than continuously injected by author, procedural clock, fitness curriculum, fixed motor tape or human manipulator? These sources can be useful experimental scaffolding but must remain attributed.
3. **Causal history:** compare physically matched afterstate with private/internal state ablation, and equal internal state with changed material World; test counterfactual **future actions** rather than only mass/trajectory difference.
4. **Ecology:** after the first minute, does local matter/regulation create *renewed possibilities* and meaningful disruptions? Does individual activity genuinely change the later environment another actor can experience? Finite scenery and a cyclic plate failed before.
5. **Open-endedness:** if repeated choices converge on a single stable attractor fixed by authored environment, do not call that open-ended adaptation, even if it can run days in accelerated time. The researcher's algorithm for searching the best attractor is not an organism's own biographical cognition.
6. **Human witness:** real browser Owner intervention and phenomenological quality remain separate; technical PASS cannot cancel explicit experiential FAIL.

## Project strategy after R6
Do not turn ReflexBrain into a second JAX/Lenia platform, and do not smuggle conservation/biology as universal obligation. Choose a bounded next primary experimental unit: **two or more distinguishable ongoing, interacting material processes with individual-linked history and a human-perturbable observation window**, while cheap purely reactive controllers and world-only controls are retained. We want a *pressure-rich source of continuing becoming*, not a conventional benchmark that optimizes one score. The choice between a genuine bottom-up structure and a hybrid anchored-to-Rapier structure remains OPEN until such a setting is physically credible; preserve earlier L1 runtime as a valuable experimental service.

This is a research route, not a claim that such an experiment already exists.
