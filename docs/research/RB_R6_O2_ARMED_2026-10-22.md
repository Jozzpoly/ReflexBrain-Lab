# R6/O2 — conservative transport is not autonomous self-maintenance (ARMED)
**2026-10-22 — before adding the deciding code/tests.** Baseline: O0/O1 original Orbium exact-source observations on [PR #87](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/ReflexBrain-Lab/pull/87).

## Source evidence; no source substitution
- [Flow-Lenia original code](https://github.com/erwanplantec/FlowLenia/blob/main/flowlenia/reintegration_tracking.py): `ReintegrationTracking._apply_without_hidden` spreads cell mass over destination cells according to overlapping local target distributions. It clamps flow `dt*F` to `dd-sigma` and target centroids to walls, or accounts for torus wraparound. The update is **redistribution**, not ordinary Lenia's `A += growth*dt`. The scalar sum should be conserved to float tolerance when distribution support is fully represented. Portable NumPy reproduction of **just this original transport operator**, no claim to reproduce full JAX Flow-Lenia morphodynamics or a specific paper creature.
- [Original Flow-Lenia parameter embedding](https://github.com/erwanplantec/FlowLenia/blob/main/flowlenia/flowlenia_params.py) returns transported `A,P`, and its optional *beam_mutation* callback explicitly injects changes from the experimental host when configured. The default `stoch` mixing in [reintegration_tracking](https://github.com/erwanplantec/FlowLenia/blob/main/flowlenia/reintegration_tracking.py) uses `jax.random.PRNGKey(42)` inside a call, fixed per call. Do **not** claim user-independent natural evolutionary novelty from this alone. Distinguish field parameter inheritance, externally supplied mutation, survival selection, and actor-internal adaptation.
- [Sensorimotor Lenia official paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12577699/), [authors' code](https://github.com/flowersteam/sensorimotor-lenia-search): robustness was obtained by diversity search, gradient descent and curriculum; 50*2000-step obstacle-rollout benchmark assesses survival; externally created "attractive element" uses extra local rule with separate search and human review. No equivalence with intrinsic goal / lasting biography.

## Frozen O2 numerical experiment
Perform **portable NumPy interpretation of the exact original single-channel reintegration law**, square 24×24 lattice, `sigma=.65, dd=3, dt=.2`, samples in double precision; grid sites centered at integer+0.5. `F` supplied by researcher and fixed over time, not endogenous field gradient. On both **wall** and **torus**, use five conditions (20 update ticks each):
- blank World with nonzero flow, should remain blank;
- one seeded field with F=0;
- same seeded field with uniform F=(2.0,-1.4);
- same seeded field with deterministic spatially varying F(x,y)=... sine/cosine;
- same seeded field with F reversed (the exact same material, opposite external vector field).
Seed field has non-negative ordinary mass at fixed positions (incl. near boundaries), total >0. Compare total initial/final mass (conservation tolerance 1e-9 absolute per update), and spatial arrangement / occupied support to show mass alone cannot determine later layout. No energy or flow-generation cost is modeled. Side by side use a standard Lenia O1 result as **qualitative contrast only**, not comparable survival scores.
- Literal discretized area/overlap operator as author source: for each relative displacement in [-dd,+dd]², roll source mass and roll destination mean by same offset; target overlap width `clip(.5-abs(dest_center-target)+sigma,0,min(1,2*sigma))` for each axis; multiply axes and divide by `4*sigma²`, sum. Include periodic wrap distances and wall centroid clipping.

## Falsifiers and limits
If mass drifts beyond 1e-9 in 20 steps, do NOT relabel the operator conservative; check boundary and support law first, and record discrepancy if unresolved. Passing sum conservation is **not** conservation of form, agency or intergenerational autonomy; the flow is intentionally externally authored and may disperse. Spatial rearrangement does not imply organism-relative significance. Only run 20 steps, one deterministic seed and one frozen field per case; not a broad proof of numerical stability.
Do not run expensive full-scale JAX curriculum, import external GPL/unknown-licence code into ReflexBrain, or quietly convert R6 into a new engine. Next research choice must ask how *source of organization/repair and hereditary variance* could be observed under long-term changing, human-perturbable ecology, controlling privileged researcher selection.
