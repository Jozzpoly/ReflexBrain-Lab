## What follows / what explicitly does NOT follow
- Two Worlds with **exactly the same total instantaneous material amount** subsequently have opposite destinies (persistence vs extinction), because the *spatial organization* of that material differs. A single global "life energy" or mass/survival scalar cannot encode this dynamical difference. Also **which** mass-conserving rearrangement was imposed matters: one survived, another killed.
- The comparison deliberately uses *different kinds of spatial perturbations*, not matched "damage severity". A 7×7 permutation could be much more disruptive locally than annular redistribution. Do NOT claim that weaker/stronger biological damage has been controlled for, nor all local permutations are lethal.

Robust O1 annular sample persisted, but no evidence it rebuilt **the same morphology**; mass by itself is not morphometric confirmation of repair.

Material appears/disappears by the prescribed field equation; a biologically meaningful material/energy economy is not established.

- The same notion of "viability" cannot be imposed blindly on the existing rigid Rapier actor (whose morphology and physical integrity are assigned by solver design).
