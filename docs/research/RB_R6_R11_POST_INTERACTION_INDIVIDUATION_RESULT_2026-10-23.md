## What NOT to infer
- The two t150 components **do not carry original organism identity**. They had a connected interval, and thresholded segmentation is host-derived. The survivor could be recomposed material in this nonlinear medium. No persistent actor self, ancestry, body membrane, metabolism, rule inheritance, learning or social ecology was demonstrated.
- The removal occurs at a favorable moment (t150 chosen AFTER reviewing R10 and explicitly pre-registered for R11). It has no population-level generality and is **not an endogenous separation process**. Claim only this one structure persists under this one host-defined extraction.
- L1 between excised and untouched jumps from ~71.70 t300 to **212.20 t400**; this is evidence of later strong field/position divergence, NOT proof that two organisms deliberately re-encounter or that a survivor has learned an avoidance strategy. Late differences can come from chaotic/oscillatory field evolution and toroidal motion; we lack a rigorously attributable actor identity through merged states.
- Source uses Chan notebook polynomial rules in our altered 128-grid/20×20/R13 fixture. [R10P](RB_R6_R10P_ORIGINAL_NUMERIC_PARITY_RESULT_2026-10-23.md) independently verifies kernel geometry and qualitatively same outcomes, **not** the author's original 256-grid `multiply_cells(4)` demo.

## Decision
This is the most concrete affirmative evidence so far that **an independently evolving material structure can survive a previous shared-field interaction and remain coherent without the other structure** — bounded sample, no original-individual identification. Continue research toward **individuation and actor-relevant history**, not a global designer fitness score, scripted return-to-material controller, another seed search or immediate Rapier/Flow-Lenia union.
