# Organism Recovery Donor Audit — 2026-10-06

Status: **RECOVERED EVIDENCE / DONOR SELECTION · NO O0 ARCHITECTURE FROZEN**

Active branch:

`experiment/organism-recovery-v0`

Purpose:

Recover the strongest actual organism-level phenomena from the pre-E0 line without importing old implementations or retrospective mythology.

This document distinguishes:
- demonstrated or strongly evidenced phenomena worth preserving;
- mechanisms that were useful only as experiments;
- artifacts and rejected interpretations;
- candidate O0 pressures that remain hypotheses.

## 1. Proto-Life switching — reject apparent life from hidden cycles

Early self-exciting/inhibitory internal-state toys produced rich-looking switching.

Later analysis showed exact or near-exact deterministic limit cycles.

Preserve lesson:

> complex switching != life.

Do not use internal activity complexity, entropy, state switching or observer surprise as an organism criterion.

## 2. Closed controller ↔ body ↔ world loop — preserve coupling, reject chaos-as-agency

Closing controller, body and world produced stronger dynamical sensitivity than the isolated components. One recovered experiment reported a positive local sensitivity around `lambda ~= +0.105`.

This was useful evidence that interesting dynamics can arise in the coupling rather than inside one "brain" module.

Rejected interpretation:
- chaotic wandering is not agency;
- unpredictability is not meaningful continuation;
- a system with nothing to lose or preserve has weak grounds for actor-relative significance.

Preserve donor principle:

> organism-level behaviour can be a property of controller ↔ body ↔ world coupling, not a property stored in one AI module.

## 3. Viability / homeostasis — preserve stakes, do not import the mechanism

A later line introduced real viability loss/recovery.

Recovered evidence:
- fixed reflex failed under sensor inversion;
- a homeostatic adaptation variant recovered roughly 89/120 inversion trials;
- under a long blackout it fell to roughly 50/120;
- blind "viability is bad -> change self" could not identify whether the fault was self, sensor, world or circumstance.

Preserve:
- consequences can matter differently because they alter an actor's future viability/possibility;
- an organism benefits from having something materially at stake.

Reject as O0 default:
- global viability scalar;
- automatic self-modification from error;
- full metabolism/homeostasis requirement.

O0 does not need viability yet unless its pressure earns it.

## 4. Reversible tester — preserve experimental logic, not actor architecture

A reversible tester tried:
- controlled local change;
- observe consequences;
- commit or revert.

It improved robustness under inverted-sensor and blackout cases, but weakened-world trials still produced false commits and deaths; one recovered campaign had 16/80 harmful false changes.

Preserve donor:
- reversible interventions are a strong **research** instrument;
- causal credit requires distinguishing self-change from world-change.

Do not promote the tester itself as Local Brain architecture.

## 5. Proto-Life v0.5 embodied negotiation — history can change later dynamics

Recovered setup:
- one simple two-DOF physical body/controller;
- Owner could drag a glowing endpoint and entrain motion;
- controller adapted coupling from recent motion correlations;
- frozen-plasticity control existed.

Recovered bounded result:
- after release, plastic variant retained substantial motion/coupling (~0.65 / ~0.82 in the recorded smoke test);
- frozen control was near inert (~0.01 / ~0).

Qualified narrow lesson:

> lived physical interaction can leave an endogenous state that changes later ordinary dynamics after the external intervention ends.

Rejected claims:
- no learned gesture concept;
- no object recognition;
- no world affordance understanding;
- no general Local Brain;
- no semantic continuity.

This is a strong **history -> later behaviour** donor, not an O0 implementation donor.

## 6. Proto-Life v0.6 Affordance Echo — preserve differentiated physical history, narrow the claim

Recovered experiment:
- same controller/body coupled to different physical objects: pendulum, wheel, spring;
- no object labels or explicit goals;
- roughly 25 s coupling followed by >20 s unplugged observation.

Recovered late plastic echo activity:
- pendulum ~2.52;
- wheel ~2.40;
- spring ~2.63.

Frozen controls were near zero:
- ~0.033;
- ~0.018;
- ~0.046.

A later broader campaign exposed a critical confound:
- LIVE no-contact control also retained substantial activity (~2.43);
- therefore "the actor learned a special echo of the object" is too strong.

What survives:

> different embodied histories can leave measurably different later internal/controller organization.

What does not survive:
- object recognition;
- useful affordance learning;
- intention;
- semantic object knowledge.

## 7. Reafference / exafference — strongest unexecuted bridge candidate

The next proposed experiment after v0.6 was:

`motor command -> predicted sensory consequence -> actual consequence -> residual/history`

The intended distinction was:
- **reafference**: change plausibly caused by the actor's own action;
- **exafference**: change not explained by current self-action, e.g. Owner/world perturbation.

This was not yet qualified as an implemented capability.

It is nevertheless highly relevant to O0 because it can make Owner physical intervention enter the organism's history without giving the actor semantic labels such as "Owner pushed me".

Treat this as a candidate pressure/mechanism to test later, not an earned architecture decision.

## 8. Persistent Playground v1 — strongest organism-shape donor, weak learner evidence

Recovered shape:
- one persistent playmat;
- one actor;
- five manipulable objects;
- local sensors and action primitives;
- prediction of sensory consequences;
- learning-progress tracking;
- experience selection biased toward improving predictability rather than raw surprise;
- Owner could drag actor/objects, take over the body, then return autonomy;
- `RESET ŚWIATA` reset material world while preserving brain;
- `RESET MÓZGU` reset brain while preserving the world;
- prediction lens exposed predicted vs actual sensory channels.

One recovered run recorded:
- 149 experiences;
- 43 visited regions;
- positive learning-progress activity.

This surface crossed an Owner "looks alive" threshold more strongly than the later E0.0 body null.

But later audit found substantial artifacts:
- wall-clinging;
- coarse regionization: 54 possible regions = 9 actions × 3 visibility states × 2 contact states;
- no object identity in the region key;
- self-motion/sound missing from the remembered key;
- Owner takeover key-repeat bug could produce zero learning;
- external manipulation could be misattributed as self-caused;
- frozen mode still created empty regions;
- debug timeline could become stale/misleading;
- no deterministic replay/RNG/full causal trace;
- some apparent life came from inertia, random motor punctuation, wall traps and UI anthropomorphism.

Therefore:

### Preserve from Playground

- persistent world/body/history;
- autonomy that continues after Owner stops touching it;
- Owner physical intervention/takeover followed by return to autonomy;
- local sensory consequences;
- observable ongoing exploration;
- separate world vs brain reset as a powerful causal microscope;
- prediction-error visibility as instrumentation.

### Do not preserve automatically

- 54-region representation;
- learning-progress chooser;
- its learner tables;
- random motor punctuation;
- wall-driven exploration;
- current prediction channels;
- UI/activity labels as evidence of life.

The Playground is a **shape donor**, not a qualified learner architecture.

## 9. L0 living-movement / continuation — preserve the problem, reject the representation

Recovered results:

First continuation version:
- 16/16 completions;
- 186 continuation changes;
- 123 changes faster than 0.25 s.

Acceleration-limited prospection:
- 15/16 completions;
- 458 continuation changes;
- 391 fast changes.

Giving continuation more authority:
- 132 changes;
- 84 fast;
- only 13/16 completions;
- 292 contacts;
- produced "sticky stupidity".

Key diagnosis:

`continuation=left` was a label attached above framewise motor recomputation. It did not itself create material continuation.

Preserve:
- continuation must survive multiple local moments and meet material outcomes;
- better prediction does not automatically produce continuity;
- more authority can trade thrash for pathological persistence;
- recovery/abandonment is as important as persistence.

Reject:
- continuation IDs as evidence;
- timers/cooldowns as the primary continuity mechanism;
- success-by-completion count alone.

## 11. Donor synthesis for O0

The strongest recovered organism-level invariants are not any one old learner.

They are:

1. **persistent actor-owned trajectory**  
   Something continues when Owner input disappears.

2. **embodied causal loop**  
   Behaviour is closed through body and world, not rendered from internal labels.

3. **lived history changes later ordinary behaviour**  
   Physical experience can matter after the immediate event ends.

4. **self/world causality is not free**  
   Owner/world perturbations must not automatically be attributed to self-action.

5. **continuation can persist, fail, recover or be abandoned**  
   Persistence alone is not intelligence.

6. **Owner can intervene without becoming the heartbeat**  
   Drag/takeover/obstruction are perturbations to an already-running life.

7. **microscope and organism remain separate**  
   Replay, branches, prediction lenses and debug truth help us understand life; they are not the life.

## 12. Current O0 hypothesis — deliberately not frozen

A promising minimal O0 is now narrower than the previous reclassification document implied.

Candidate core:

- one persistent actor in a materially richer but still small world;
- E0-derived body/contact substrate;
- one cheap autonomous activity/exploration loop;
- local prediction of immediate sensory/body consequences;
- persistent private history of outcomes;
- at least one material obstruction requiring continuation/recovery;
- Owner can move an object or perturb/take over and then release;
- actor later behaves differently only when its own legal history supports it.

The strongest early falsifier should not be "does it look alive?"

It should be:

> after a controlled lived perturbation and a washout interval, can later ordinary behaviour differ for a causal reason stored in actor-private history, while matched irrelevant intervention does not produce the same change?

This is compatible with the broader causal-life criterion:

`lived event -> endogenous state change -> later ordinary behaviour`.

Do not implement the full candidate yet. First red-team whether this O0 would merely recreate Playground bookkeeping with better physics.
