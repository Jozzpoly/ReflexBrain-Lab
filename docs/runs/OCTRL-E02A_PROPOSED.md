## Researcher-only accessibility audit

Passage accessibility must not be a World semantic bit.

The run may define one fixed **clearance radius** for measurement.

Research instrumentation may ask whether that clearance can geometrically traverse the declared local passage in a snapshot.

The measurement:
- cannot influence physics;
- cannot drive the shuttle;
- cannot move the blocker;
- cannot become actor-facing state.

E02a claim is relative to that declared research clearance only.

It does not yet establish actual B0/B1 traversability.

## Evidence plan

Need one deterministic causal chain:

`E01 process -> physical blocker displacement -> researcher-measured passage state changes -> changed physical state persists after immediate contact`

Preferred direction may be:
- open -> blocked,
or
- blocked -> open.

Either is acceptable if caused by ordinary physics.

Persistence must be checked after the immediate interaction, not at the collision instant only.

### Control

Matched world with the independent E01 process disabled, or a matched configuration where the blocker is not physically displaced.

The control must not show the same passage-state transition.

## Falsifier

E02a is **FAIL** if:

- a second authored mechanism is required;
- the blocker must be teleported/scripted;
- semantic open/closed state is required for the effect;
- the E01 process is not causally responsible for the measured passage-state change;
- the changed passage state does not persist beyond immediate contact;
- useful composition requires actor cognition/perception/task logic.

E02a is **INCONCLUSIVE** if:
- passage accessibility cannot be measured independently of the dynamics;
- the chosen clearance definition is ambiguous enough that blocked/open cannot be distinguished;
- instrumentation contamination prevents causal attribution.
