> world-first spatial interaction is desirable, but a completely opaque canvas surface is difficult to test, inspect, automate and make accessible.

> can physical world entities expose contextual interaction semantics/hit targets without visually becoming a dashboard?

- DOM/SVG world entities rather than canvas-only hit testing;
