## Purpose

Choose the smallest ecology mechanism set that creates genuine independent causality, persistent material change and partial-observability pressure without becoming a task-specific workshop or timer puzzle.

This is a mechanism selection, not a final map.

# Selected first ecology family: M0 Dynamic Mechanical Yard

## Core principle

Use a very small number of **physically legible, locally lawful mechanisms** whose interactions can rearrange material state without the focal actor or Owner.

The world should create consequences through composition, not through authored scenario phases.

---

# Mechanism M0-A — bounded mechanical shuttle / sweeper

A dynamic body is constrained to one simple physical path/rail.

It receives continuous bounded motor force.

It reverses because it encounters **physical end conditions**, not because a global timer says "phase 2".

Properties:

- keeps moving without focal actor;
- can collide with loose bodies;
- can displace them;
- can be slowed or stalled by physical obstruction;
- its actual phase/position is not supplied to actor as hidden state;
- collisions alter immediate trajectory/outcome;
- initial position/direction can vary across seeds.

Research value:

- exogenous motion;
- off-screen material change;
- matched self/world sensory displacement;
- predictable but not clock-oracle dynamics;
- interaction-dependent process.

Anti-shortcut controls:

- vary initial position/velocity;
- vary motor strength within bounded range;
- perturb with movable bodies;
- same elapsed time can yield different shuttle state;
- same shuttle position can occur at different elapsed times.

No actor policy may access global tick or shuttle phase.

---

# Mechanism M0-B — local material topology relation

One local physical relation can change access/topology.

Candidate form:

- a plate/lever/weight relation controls a gate/barrier;
- relation depends on current local material configuration;
- loose bodies or actor contact can alter it;
- changed state persists while physical condition persists.

Important:

The actor does not perceive:
`gateOpen=true`
as semantic truth.

It perceives:
- local geometry;
- visible motion/configuration;
- contact;
- perhaps auditory event later.

Research value:

- topology can become stale in memory;
- actor can accidentally or deliberately restructure future access;
- sweeper can indirectly alter passage by moving a body;
- causal chains can cross mechanisms.

Anti-fixture rule:

The relation must be reusable in multiple layouts and not exist solely to solve the first authored concern.

---

# Loose material set

Include a small set of dynamic bodies with controlled physical variation.

Minimum useful dimensions:

- at least two masses/effectivities;
- at least two visible appearance signatures;
- some visually similar bodies may differ physically in later tests.

No semantic actor labels:
- heavy;
- useful;
- obstacle;
- token;
- key.

Researcher/world may know exact properties.

Actor learns/uses only legal evidence.

---

# Qualitative spatial structure

Do not freeze coordinates.

Required properties:

- open area large enough to assess locomotion;
- at least one occluded side area;
- at least one loop / alternate local route;
- one chokepoint whose accessibility can change;
- shuttle path intersects material traffic but does not dominate entire map;
- enough room for loose bodies to accumulate in materially different configurations.

Target:
2–4 understandable regions, not a large map.

---

# Why M0 is stronger than "bigger map"

It creates:

### independent causality
shuttle continues without actor.

### persistent history
objects remain where processes/contacts move them.

### partial observability
changes can occur behind occlusion.

### compositional consequences
shuttle -> loose body -> topology relation -> changed access.

### actor controllability
actor can push/move some bodies and thereby alter later state.

### multiple futures
same local state can evolve differently depending on material configuration/intervention.

### timescale separation
fast contact;
medium shuttle/topology changes;
slow accumulation/rearrangement of loose material.

# Main red-team risks

## R1 — mechanical theme park

The ecology could still be a puzzle machine.

Defense:
- no scene-specific solution;
- mechanisms reusable across rearrangements;
- no policy access to mechanism role;
- Owner can legally rearrange loose bodies and layout.

## R2 — shuttle timing code

Defense:
physical end-state reversal and interaction-dependent motion; variable initial state.

## R3 — gate relation becomes "key puzzle"

Defense:
do not require the first concern to use the gate.
It is an ecology process, not a task step.

## R4 — too much physics

Defense:
only two mechanism families.
No fluids, destruction, complex joints, procedural generation.

## R5 — actor does not care

Correct.
Ecology is not normativity.

The first occupant supplies a scoped trajectory source.

---

# Held-out rearrangement principle

The same mechanism code should support:

- different wall/chokepoint arrangement;
- shuttle rail placed in another region/orientation;
- topology relation attached to a different passage;
- different loose-body initial states.

Actor policy receives no layout ID.

Owner may create one legal rearrangement not pre-authored by policy author.
