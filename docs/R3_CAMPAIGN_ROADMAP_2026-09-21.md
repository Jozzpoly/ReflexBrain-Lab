# ReflexBrain R3+ Campaign Roadmap — 2026-09-21

Status: **LONG CAMPAIGN MAP · SEQUENCE IS CONDITIONAL, NOT MECHANICAL**

This roadmap exists so short Owner instructions such as `kontynuuj` can advance the project without losing the larger research direction.

It is not permission to execute every box in order.

At every gate, evidence may:
- reorder the campaign;
- split a phase;
- invalidate a hypothesis;
- require a donor audit;
- delete a scaffold;
- stop model work and return to the host.

### Product-claim authority

Owner-observed behavior governs claims about life, feel, usefulness and product function. CI and research harnesses may qualify narrower evidence only; they cannot overrule an Owner FAIL.

R3 zero-input autonomy is therefore substrate evidence, not proof that the fixture residents are living/product-ready.

## Campaign objective

Reach a point where a small local learned mechanism demonstrably adds actor-relative semantic/reflex competence inside autonomous life and generalizes beyond the ecology that produced its training evidence.

The first Owner test is deliberately downstream of that achievement.

## Phase A — legitimate actor-private continuity — **QUALIFIED 2026-09-21**

**Purpose:** remove semantic context that currently exists only in the research runner.

Work:
- introduce minimal actor-owned continuing matters/purposes;
- expose them through the local private-state boundary;
- record them in experience from the resident, not from runner metadata;
- preserve multiple matters in the contract without inventing a final scheduler;
- keep matter lifecycle minimal.

Pass gate:
- both autonomous ecologies remain green;
- corpus can be reconstructed using only actor-private state + separately held factual outcomes;
- changing one actor's matter changes only that actor's private semantic context;
- no World snapshot or runner-only purpose enters inference input.

Red flags:
- copying SPC's full matter kernel by habit;
- adding salience/priority because a scheduler needs a number;
- making one `primaryGoal` the permanent architecture.

## Phase B — substrate/corpus falsification — **QUALIFIED LIMITS, NO SEMANTIC TARGET PROMOTED**

**Purpose:** make training/evaluation data reflect life rather than scenario labels.

Work:
- collect temporal windows from both ecologies;
- classify fields by provenance: exact private fact / private memory / authored matter / future factual outcome;
- add leakage controls for actor identity, absolute tick, place names and fixture-specific tokens;
- create train/held-out splits across ecology, role, event and policy families;
- add negative controls capable of solving by surface tokens;
- distinguish intervention-derived causal provenance from semantic target authority;
- require policy-wiring invariance when a label is claimed to express model-visible semantic meaning;
- require semantic-content identifiability: permuting candidate statement meaning must not leave a supposedly semantic label arbitrarily attached to authored ids;
- preserve isolated examples where the same event reaches actors with different matters.

Pass gate:
- held-out ecology contains legitimate unseen relationships;
- simple surface/identity/context-only/matter-only baselines fail where semantic representation should succeed;
- a claimed semantic label does not flip under irrelevant hidden fixture rewiring that leaves model-visible private life and candidate meaning unchanged;
- a claimed semantic target is actually sensitive to the semantic content it purports to supervise, rather than only to matter identity or policy binding;
- actor/domain/policy-family holdouts distinguish transfer from fixture memorization;
- temporal windows do not leak future outcomes.

Red flags:
- hand-labeling OOD failures back into TRAIN;
- tuning exact phrases to improve held-out score;
- treating intervention provenance as semantic truth without an invariance test;
- attempting to recover semantic supervision from a statement-blind causal generator merely by adding corpus size, balance or model capacity;
- treating corpus size as progress.

### Active Phase B finding — statement-blind causal labels cannot supervise statement meaning

The current R3 fixture policies consume matter identity but not `matter.statement` semantics.

A statement-permutation metamorphic falsifier (Check #209, 19 files / 162 tests PASS) swaps the semantic statements between fixed matter ids while preserving the observed/memory/decision trajectory and the ablation-responsible id.

Therefore the present ID-ablation label family is closed as semantic supervision in this substrate. Phase B must now discover a target/objective with a legitimate semantic grounding path before Phase C model work resumes. Counterbalancing the old target is no longer sufficient.

## Phase B.5 — semantic consumer qualification — **ACTIVE · FIRST BOUNDED CONSUMER FAMILY QUALIFIED**

**Purpose:** prove that an ideal actor-private semantic judgement has a useful downstream role before choosing a learner to approximate it.

This restores the campaign's oracle-before-learned-replacement discipline without returning to the rejected R2 clicker.

Work:
- choose one bounded semantic distinction that could affect ordinary autonomous life;
- define an ideal research oracle over legitimate actor-private evidence;
- route the oracle through one explicit deterministic consumer/executive seam;
- keep World/body authority outside the oracle;
- run zero-input paired A/B under identical exogenous conditions;
- compare oracle against null and simple non-semantic baselines;
- measure causal consequences such as continuity, relevant response, unnecessary interruption, recovery or task loss.

Pass gate:
- the ideal semantic advisory produces a material, repeatable causal benefit unavailable to the null/simple baseline;
- the benefit survives a qualitatively different pressure case or counterfactual;
- the oracle does not need hidden World truth;
- the consumer remains useful when separated from the oracle implementation;
- no Owner/product claim is promoted from machine evidence alone.

### Qualified first consumer family

At head `3cfff490e9ee02331ace8787aa450be7954320af`, a report-applicability consumer passed the first bounded oracle gate:

- same matter ids, same pressure timeline, private purpose meaning changed;
- ideal joint semantic relation: 1.000;
- fixed surface controls: 0.500;
- persistent required reports repeat when unresolved;
- oracle resolves all required pressure with 50 acknowledgements;
- respond-all also resolves pressure but spends 150 acknowledgements;
- ignore-all leaves 200 Ida speech events versus oracle/respond-all 150;
- fixed-surface rules leave 175 Ida speech events and resolve only half the required reports across both purpose meanings;
- throughput remains equal at 22 processing completions.

This qualifies **bounded causal consumer value**, not a learned mechanism and not Phase B.5 generality.

Remaining Phase B.5 work:
- survive at least one qualitatively different semantic-pressure family or counterfactual;
- prove the useful boundary is not an artifact of exact authored text pairs;
- keep oracle outputs evaluation/instrumentation until supervision provenance is separately justified.

Fail gate:
- if a broader pressure family removes the semantic advantage, narrow the claim instead of promoting the consumer contract;
- if even ideal semantic information has no meaningful downstream consequence in a proposed family, reject or reformulate that semantic-output family before model work.

Boundary:
- oracle answers are research instrumentation;
- they are not automatic training labels;
- the oracle shape is not the final ReflexBrain API;
- no Owner-facing clicker is required.

## Phase C — representation probes, not product brain

**Purpose:** only after Phase B.5 demonstrates a useful consumer boundary and the evaluation pressure is hardened against surface memorization, ask what small representation/learning objective can approximate the required actor-private semantic distinction.

Initial candidates, selected only after the consumer boundary is known:
- qualified R1 MiniLM frozen encoder;
- exact actor-private structured channels that should not be inferred from prose;
- simple transparent probes;
- relational, temporal-difference, contrastive or predictive/self-supervised objectives where their target matches the demonstrated consumer need.

Questions:
- Can the same event be represented differently when the actor's matter/context differs?
- Can semantically similar relevance relationships transfer across ecologies?
- Does adding short private history improve transfer over single-frame input?
- Which distinctions need exact structured channels?

Pass gate:
- meaningful held-out cross-ecology signal;
- stronger than lexical/identity controls;
- no hidden-World leakage;
- stable under irrelevant batch/layout changes.

Red flags:
- resurrecting five scores because infrastructure exists;
- larger model before representation failure is understood;
- MLP used merely to fit the corpus.

## Phase D — discover the output contract

**Purpose:** learn what ReflexBrain should emit from downstream evidence rather than naming outputs first.

Candidate families to test, not assume:
- event-to-matter relation;
- semantic applicability/currentness;
- expectation violation / novelty;
- evidence that a continuing local activity deserves reconsideration;
- uncertainty that deeper cognition may resolve;
- compact latent relation state rather than independent scalars.

Method:
- inspect which learned distinctions consistently predict useful downstream changes across ecologies;
- compare explicit narrow factors versus learned latent representation;
- retain deterministic downstream policy during this phase.

Pass gate:
- output contract is supported by multiple ecologies and counterfactuals;
- each output has a concrete consumer or diagnostic meaning;
- removing an output measurably loses useful discrimination.

## Phase E — temporal Reflex Dynamics

**Purpose:** avoid a frame-by-frame classifier that thrashes.

Questions:
- what should persist?
- what should decay?
- what should habituate?
- when does new evidence override old local appraisal?
- when is memory/context enough without rerunning semantics?

Compare:
- stateless learned readout;
- explicit deterministic temporal dynamics;
- learned temporal state;
- hybrid.

Pass gate:
- continuity improves without hiding errors;
- repeated/noisy stimuli do not cause pathological flip-flop;
- state remains actor-private and inspectable enough to debug causally.

## Phase F — shadow insertion into autonomous life

**Purpose:** run the learned candidate continuously without authority.

The candidate observes the same private stream as the actor and emits shadow outputs.

Measure:
- first divergence from deterministic local policy;
- latency/cost;
- cross-ecology consistency;
- reactions to natural pressure;
- failures under stale beliefs, moving contacts, blocked work and shared speech;
- whether output is redundant with cheap rules.

Pass gate:
- candidate adds useful information not already trivially available;
- failures are interpretable enough to continue;
- local cost is plausible for frequent use.

## Phase G — bounded causal authority experiment

Only after shadow evidence.

Introduce a tiny explicit executive seam where the learned mechanism may influence one bounded choice, while World remains authoritative.

Required controls:
- null/no-reflex;
- deterministic baseline;
- learned candidate;
- identical exogenous conditions;
- first-divergence logging;
- factual outcome comparison.

Success means better causal behavior, not nicer scores.

## Phase H — broaden life pressure

Only now add qualitatively new pressure if needed:
- conflicting matters;
- stale instructions;
- social applicability;
- hazards;
- uncertain evidence;
- concurrent actors;
- environmental change.

Do not grow a feature zoo. Add a pressure family only if it can falsify a live hypothesis.

## Phase I — Owner-test candidate

An Owner surface is earned only when there is a real capability to feel/test.

Minimum preconditions:
- autonomous world continues without Owner input;
- learned mechanism is present;
- it generalizes beyond its training ecology;
- it has at least one bounded causal role or a clearly visible shadow competence;
- the Owner can perturb a living world rather than advance it manually;
- UI does not manufacture the behavior being evaluated.

The Owner test is then evidence about feel, agency and emergent competence — not a replacement for implementation.
