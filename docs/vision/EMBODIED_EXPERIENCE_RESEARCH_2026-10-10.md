## 2. Principal correction — stop treating image resolution as the real problem
A small PNG, 24x24 map, 96-ray retina, vector stream, event packet or learned latent state is a **representation choice**, not an intelligence mechanism.

The deeper question is:
> Under real, independently evolving physical/social conditions, can an actor with lawful, limited, fallible perception and private sensorimotor memory learn/use action-relevant distinctions that change its own continuing behavior, without omniscient labels, researcher-supplied meaning, or unjustified control authority?

Qualifying this requires at least three questions that must not be conflated:
1. **What information can this particular body lawfully acquire?** (sensing and perspective)
2. **What is this actor justified in believing from that information and its history?** (private memory, uncertainty, assumptions)
3. **When does an observed discrepancy actually change the value/feasibility of an actor's ongoing action?** (actor-relative competence and significance)

Physical possibility is not the same as a perceptual cue. Surprise is not the same as incompetence. Incompetence is not yet a reason. A reason is not automatically motor authority.

## 4. Five independent resolutions — no universal LOD ladder
| Layer | What may be approximated | What must not be inferred from approximation |
|---|---|---|
| World simulation | distant entity/activity and physical detail, subject to explicit causality contract | unknown exact microhistory that never ran |
| Lawful sensors | retina FOV, frequency, spatial detail, sound/touch | hidden World truth, authoritative object IDs |
| Private memory | event selection, temporal compression, uncertain latent models | remembered = currently visible, or low residual = certainty |
| Local cognition | compute frequency, deliberation depth, routing | low compute = no ongoing actor concern, high compute = autonomous life |
| Analyst/game display | camera crop, overlays, causal trace, authoring tools | debug map or researcher event clock = actor experience |

Important asymmetry: **world scale/distance and actor significance are not aligned**. An offscreen event could be causally important; a nearby visible wall might require no cognition. A coarse world simulation can produce consistent macro consequences but must not retroactively invent exact private perceptions or actual event provenance.

## 5. Scientific rival families (not competing products)
A. Structured P0/contact/proprioception/private history — strong low-cost baseline.
B. Directional retina/rays/sectors with active gaze — existing Living Organism donor.
C. Actor-local fixed spatial raster, legal sampled channels/masks — useful if it preserves spatial relations otherwise lost.
D. Near-detail plus far-coarse / log-polar multiscale — additional information and sampling cost must be accounted for; do not claim a coding win from increased range.
E. Sparse temporal sensory changes + direct contacts + proprioception/history — may outperform dense time stacks where change is informative.
F. Learned compressed/belief representation produced from lawful history — may preserve predictive/action-relevant distinctions, but can create invisible failure and hidden privilege leaks.

No requirement for the **organism** to store/render a PNG; human evidence captures and machine runtime format are separate products. A persistent complete map is an option, not a guaranteed needed organ.

A fair experimental comparison requires:
- **matched legal information**: representation differences under the same sensor coverage, cadence and history;
- **added legal information**: a separate accounting when fields, FOV, range, cadence or modality are increased;
- measured wall-clock/compute, allocations, sampling costs and actor downstream causal changes (not simply MAE/masks);
- held-out ecology/body/geometry, movement-induced view changes, occlusion and sensory age;
- no implicit world ID, exact hidden contact, authored `threat`/`passable` label, or oracle affordance in actor features.

## 6. The stronger hypothesis: affordance is a relation, not a painted map channel
The same visual configuration can support different actions for a small creature and a large armored body. Even the same body may have a different capability after injury, loading, fatigue or changed contact conditions. Its own history, proprioception and physical outcome may update expectations.

But a `passable` raster would often smuggle in the answer. The sensor may report lawful appearance/contact/proprioception; the actor-specific **possibility to pass/push/avoid** is a learned/derived belief requiring evaluation. Do not rename collision geometry as learned affordance.

Likewise, the same mismatch can be irrelevant if the actor is simply passing by and consequential if it disrupts an actor-originated ongoing activity. Be careful not to hardcode R6's Matter/Concern labels as learning targets: the actor-owned relation's *origin* is part of the question.

Possible high-value distinction:
- `assumption failed`: prediction/causal model mismatch;
- `action loss`: a modeled option actually performs worse than another (research ORACLE may evaluate, actor does not see oracle);
- `ongoing relation`: prior actor activity or legitimate private relation makes the difference consequential;
- `information action`: the actor chooses lawful observation/motion to resolve an uncertainty because it may improve continuation.

These are **questions**, not four canonical brain fields.

## 7. Four adversarial worlds to distinguish hypotheses
1. **Same sight, different bodies:** narrow physically reachable passage for the small body, untraversable for the larger. The observed geometry can be held matched while private morphology/effectivity differs. Do not provide the `can-pass` label. Compare actual attempts/afterstates.
2. **Same current sight, different private history:** actor previously attempted a material action with a specific outcome versus merely seeing the same place. A new obstacle/current percept may deserve re-evaluation in one history only. **Do not inject a host-authored goal during test**.
3. **Hidden change with legal reacquisition:** extend G5A *conceptually*: the world changes while occluded, actor does not know; later gaze/body motion can produce legal new evidence. Compare passive recurring samples with physically grounded active information acquisition. Authored timer-triggered CHECK is a control, not evidence of learned curiosity.
4. **Image alias and motion parallax:** same initial retinal appearance produced by different sizes/distances/relative motion; actor can take a lateral motion/gaze action to disambiguate where feasible. Moving object can invalidate static assumption even at low residual. Compare actual downstream decision quality, not only range estimate.

Feniks's **2.5D verticality** is a future adversarial transfer target: something may be visually blocked, physically walkable beneath, projectile-reachable above, or socially forbidden despite physical access. An exact flat occupancy crop is not a universal description of that scenario. No full 2.5D host is authorized by this note.

## 8. Biggest failure modes of the vision program
- Enriching microscopes while actors remain authored puppets.
- Building 24x24→64x64→... map levels merely because they are easy to visualize.
- Scoring image reconstruction/prediction improvement as organism competence.
- Treating epistemic uncertainty as an objective to minimize regardless of actor context (pointless looking).
- Treating every surprise as an interruption trigger (F2/P0 disproves this shortcut).
- Encoding World privileged state as sensor channels, or treating occluded = empty.
- Training on narrow appearances/colors and then calling it reusable meaning.
- Supposing dense arrays are cheap because bytes are small; raycasts, transforms, update cadence and learning matter.
- Letting world LOD destroy invisible causal continuity then manufacturing fine-grained event history on refinement.
- Assuming a major social/normative judgement is a direct visual feature.
- Hiding failure via hand-picked camera views, trajectory replays, or one successfully animated NPC.

## 11. Red-team correction: looking and moving are often the same physical act
The active-sensing hypothesis has a **major attribution trap**. If an actor steps sideways to inspect a passage and later crosses it successfully, the step might have:
- revealed useful visual information;
- simply moved the body closer to the correct approach path;
- changed the physical scene through contact;
- improved the authored controller's geometry even if the retina was ignored.

Thus "looked, then succeeded" does **not** prove that information acquisition improved its competence, much less that its motivation was self-generated.

A future *diagnostic* 2x2 contrast should try to separate:
1. **Same physical movement, fresh lawful observation:** sensory channel available as specified.
2. **Same physical movement, withheld newly revealed information:** actor continues on the pre-intervention cached frame/declared occlusion, while World mechanics are held identical up to decision boundary.
3. **Passive/no probing action, normal observation:** control for whether active exploration was necessary.
4. **Matched-cost noninformative movement:** control for extra time, energy, contact and coincidental relocation.

The resulting continuation may diverge physically **after** the informational boundary. Do not demand impossible equivalence then; declare the matched pre-branch causal substrate, all controlled channels and remaining unknowns. A log lacking an event is not proof that no event happened if coverage is incomplete.

Outcome evidence required: observed private belief/action differences *after* lawful information; identifiable difference in subsequent material consequences; alternative explanation and blind spot accounting. If the extra view helps only an authored information trigger, report **host/controller improvement**, not learned curiosity. If observation changes nothing consequential, retain negative result.

Further falsifier: a policy that constantly scans everything can win raw information gain while worsening the actor's real ongoing activity. **Information amount and information value are different quantities**, and latter is situated, not an unconditional salience scalar.
