# Material affordance D1 — pre-experiment protocol

Question: Can an actor infer the useful consequence of pushing two identically colored and shaped bodies from its own proprioception and touch, without access to host collider identity?

Compare anchored and movable matter, initially identical in the native 96-ray RGB retina. Evaluate the physical displacement of matter on the host side only. The learner may see its own body-relative odometry, touch pressure and motor effort. Include always-push and never-push controls, then attempt held-out variations in distance, mass and marker appearance. A visual marker must earn relevance through earlier personal physical experience; it must not be hard-coded as a reward.

No promotion to production from test fixture success. Preserve physically indistinguishable material as an explicit epistemic negative control.
