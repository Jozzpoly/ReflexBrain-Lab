# 1. Strong positive interaction donors

## Proto-Life v0.5 — physical entrainment, then release

Recovered:
- Owner could drag a glowing endpoint and physically entrain a simple two-DOF body/controller;
- the controller adapted from recent motion correlation;
- after release, the plastic variant retained substantial motion/coupling while frozen control was near inert.

Medium lesson:

> **intervention -> release -> later changed ordinary dynamics** is materially more valuable than intervention -> immediate scripted reaction.

The important surface property is not "dragging".

It is that Owner intervention can enter lived history and still matter **after Owner stops touching the system**.

This strongly supports making RELEASE a first-class experimental transition in future Field surfaces.

---

## Persistent Playground v1 — persistent playmat + takeover/release

Recovered shape:
- one persistent playmat;
- one actor;
- five manipulable objects;
- local sensors and action primitives;
- Owner could drag actor and objects;
- Owner could **take over the body, then return autonomy**;
- `RESET ŚWIATA` reset material World while preserving brain;
- `RESET MÓZGU` reset brain while preserving World;
- prediction lens exposed predicted vs actual sensory channels.

One recorded run:
- 149 experiences;
- 43 visited regions;
- positive learning-progress activity.

This crossed an Owner "looks alive / worth pursuing" threshold more strongly than later clean body nulls.

Medium lesson candidates:

### A. direct material manipulation
Owner should be able to alter the same physical substrate the actor inhabits.

### B. takeover / release
Owner temporarily becoming motor authority can be an unusually strong perturbation, **provided the actor is not told semantically that Owner took over**.

The interesting question begins on release:
- what private history remains?
- what did the actor misattribute to self?
- what ordinary behavior changes later?

### C. orthogonal resets
"same brain, new World" and "same World, new brain" are powerful causal interventions.

This is more informative than one generic RESET because it can separate:
- learned/private history effects;
- material-history effects;
- fresh initial-condition effects.

### D. persistent ordinary trajectory
The actor/world should already be doing something before Owner enters and should continue after Owner stops.

Without this, direct manipulation degenerates into a clicker.

# 3. Interaction patterns currently supported by evidence

These are **research hypotheses for medium design**, not frozen UX requirements.

## Pattern 1 — Perturb an already-running life

Preferred:

`autonomous trajectory -> Owner perturbation -> Owner release -> autonomous continuation`

Weak substitute:

`Owner click -> scripted event -> result`

## Pattern 2 — Preserve consequences beyond intervention

A useful perturbation should have a chance to change:
- material state;
- private history;
- later ordinary behavior;

rather than only produce transient animation.

## Pattern 3 — Give Owner causal cuts, not semantic cheats

High-value examples:
- same World / reset private brain;
- same brain / reset World;
- pause actor / leave ecology running;
- disable one causal channel;
- fork exact current state.

Dangerous shortcut:
- "make actor forget target" if the actual future organism has no such direct causal seam.

Medium cuts should correspond to real research boundaries.

## Pattern 4 — Direct manipulation is necessary but not sufficient

Dragging objects is valuable only if:
- physical consequences are real;
- world/actor continue after release;
- intervention does not become hidden actor knowledge;
- later state can be inspected/reproduced.

## Pattern 5 — The Owner needs both ignorance and microscope

Two useful experiences:
- **stand beside the organism** and see only enough to form intuitions;
- **open the organism under a microscope** to attack those intuitions.

A single always-on telemetry dashboard destroys the first.
A pure visual playground destroys the second.

## Pattern 6 — Separate World history from private history

Historical separate World/brain resets are especially valuable because ReflexBrain's entire scientific question depends on this separation.

The medium should eventually support exact causal comparisons around that boundary.

# 5. What current Field Lab v0 gets wrong / does not yet know

## Confirmed issue: Actor Lens is not epistemically pure

Current visual Actor Lens still renders World-side geometry/process bodies that are not part of the actor's P0.

Therefore it is a visualization mode, not yet a truthful private projection.

Do not patch by hiding arbitrary objects until the desired semantics of Actor Lens are defined.

## Dashboard gravity

Current v0 exposes many controls/cards beside the World.

Risk:
the medium becomes a control panel with a specimen embedded inside.

Need to test whether:
- the world remains visually primary;
- instrumentation can be progressively disclosed;
- Owner can simply watch without reading telemetry.

## Weak ordinary activity

Current authored actor still has a simple ROAM / bounce / stale-evidence CHECK loop.

It may produce movement without producing an interesting trajectory.

Do not solve this with:
- more random turning;
- more states;
- more UI labels.

This is primarily organism pressure, not medium polish.

## Material ecology is spatially partitioned

Current independent sweeper occupies a lower lane and affects the target only when Owner deliberately puts material into it.

This is useful as an engineering donor but may be too workshop-like / ornamental.

Need research before integration.

## No experiment continuity

A page reset/tab closure loses the lived state.

No exact save/fork/revisit yet.

Do not claim "come back later" until this exists.

## No A/B from lived moment

Owner cannot yet say:
- "this moment is interesting";
- fork it;
- alter one thing;
- run both histories.

This is likely more valuable than many additional intervention buttons.

# 6. MEDIUM-A questions before Field v0.2

Do not implement answers yet.

1. **Takeover:** Is temporary Owner motor takeover worth restoring as a research instrument? What private causal evidence must remain so it tests self/world attribution rather than merely being fun?
2. **Reset duality:** What does "reset brain" mean for current/future O-CTRL/O-DEV without inventing a fake monolithic brain boundary?
3. **Actor Lens:** Should it show raw P0 only, private reconstructed history, or multiple selectable actor-private layers?
4. **World richness:** How little ecology can still generate Owner-created questions without becoming a contrived workshop?
5. **Forking:** What is the smallest exact state needed to branch a meaningful history?
6. **Return:** Should first continuity milestone be explicit save/load, session slots, or deterministic snapshots? Do not simulate closed-tab time by pretending.
7. **Observation:** Which information should be invisible by default so Owner can form an unaided intuition before opening microscope?
8. **Long run:** Which failures only emerge after thousands of ticks, and how should the medium surface them without becoming telemetry soup?
9. **Interventions:** Which interventions are genuinely material and open-ended versus scenario buttons in disguise?
10. **Evidence promotion:** How does an Owner discovery become a reproducible future falsifier without burdening Owner with manual experiment authoring?
