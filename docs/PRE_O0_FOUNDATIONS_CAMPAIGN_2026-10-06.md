## Campaign purpose

Before any broad/final organism implementation, re-open the foundations of the ReflexBrain organism problem.

The campaign asks:

> What is the smallest artificial organism/world coupling rich enough that actor-private meaning, continuity, learning and later ReflexBrain pressure can arise for non-trivial causal reasons rather than from authored labels, task phases, debug truth or impoverished toy geometry?

The campaign must be willing to invalidate:
- the maintenance-site ecology;
- the one-actor assumption;
- the current body-controller choice;
- structured object perception;
- symbolic last-known memory;
- authored concern;
- Local Brain layering;
- explicit continuation objects;
- the three-scene generalization test;
- even the current decomposition of Host organism / Local Brain / ReflexBrain if evidence demands a better boundary.

The long-term ReflexBrain research question remains alive. This campaign exists to construct a better substrate on which that question is meaningful.

# 1. What counts as an organism-level phenomenon?

Do not begin from implementation modules.

Recover observable properties.

Candidate organism signatures:

### A. trajectory ownership
With Owner absent, the actor continues a history that appears to belong to the actor rather than the UI/script.

### B. endogenous normativity
Some world changes are better/worse/different for the actor because of its own continuing organization, not because a test labels them positive or negative.

### C. interactional asymmetry
The actor changes its coupling with the environment; it is not merely pushed through a prepared sequence.

### D. sensorimotor self
The system can exploit lawful regularities between its own motor output and resulting sensory/body change.

### E. private epistemic continuity
Current action can depend on what this actor has and has not experienced, including stale or wrong private knowledge.

### F. adaptive continuity
Perturbations can alter later ordinary behaviour without replacing the organism with a new script.

### G. bounded generalization
The same competence survives materially rearranged worlds without scene-specific policy edits.

### H. open causal future
The organism is not merely replaying one short authored cycle; independent world processes and perturbations create multiple possible continuations.

None of these alone is "life".

The goal is to identify a conjunction sufficient for a useful research organism.

# 2. Ecology before brain

The old small empty arenas may have constrained the project more severely than the brain mechanisms did.

A causally poor world can only generate poor actor-relative distinctions.

Research questions:

- What minimum number of independent causal processes is needed before history becomes useful rather than decorative?
- Does a static obstacle course create genuine organism pressure or only navigation pressure?
- How much spatial heterogeneity is needed?
- Do we need occlusion, irreversible changes, moving entities, resource cycles, temporal processes, other actors, or only some subset?
- Which independent processes create naturally competing continuations?
- Can the world surprise the actor without random-noise theatre?
- What world changes remain consequential after the actor leaves and returns?

Candidate ecology ingredients to compare, not combine automatically:

1. **material maintenance ecology**  
   recurrent physical process / source / site;

2. **dynamic environmental ecology**  
   doors, moving objects, fields, changing passages, periodic processes;

3. **foraging/viability ecology**  
   body-state consequences and recoverable resources;

4. **play/exploration ecology**  
   objects with different lawful sensorimotor contingencies;

5. **multi-actor ecology**  
   independent causal lines from another autonomous body;

6. **hybrid micro-world**  
   one maintenance pressure plus one independent non-task process.

Strong anti-goal:

> do not increase map size while leaving causal dimensionality almost unchanged.

# 3. Body and morphology

E0 demonstrated a bounded physical body seam, not a final body.

Open questions:

- Is a symmetric disc too causally poor for meaningful affordances?
- Does orientation matter enough if shape does not?
- Would even a minimal asymmetric body produce useful body-scaled affordances?
- Should carry/manipulation alter morphology or inertia?
- Does body compliance/slip/traction create useful information without becoming simulation fetish?
- What forms of passive/morphological computation can reduce Local Brain complexity?
- Is direct force/torque demand the right stable seam?

Competing body hypotheses:

### B0 — preserve E0 disc
Use it until a concrete organism failure appears.

### B1 — asymmetric oriented body
Capsule / wedge / nose-body with orientation-relevant collision.

### B2 — minimal articulated/contact body
Only if manipulation/body-world coupling requires it.

Do not choose based on visual appeal.

Choose based on whether morphology produces qualitatively useful action-scaled distinctions.

# 4. Perception: do not smuggle ontology

Yesterday's structured vision may already encode too much.

Open questions:

- What does "visible entity identity" mean before the actor has learned object permanence?
- Does giving `kind=material` pre-solve categorization?
- Does exact bearing + distance become a hidden navigation sensor?
- Is stable identity an authored world handle, a perceptual track, or a belief?
- Should vision expose objects at all, or only local geometric/appearance features?
- How much perception richness is needed before learning is possible?
- Can a middle layer separate computer-vision difficulty from cognitive difficulty without giving semantic answers?

Candidate perception levels:

### P0 — geometric sensorium
Egocentric rays/segments, contact, proprioception, no object identities.

### P1 — perceptual tracklets
Local percept clusters with temporary track identity, approximate relative pose, simple physical signature; no semantic kind.

### P2 — structured object percepts
Stable object IDs/kinds after visibility.

### P3 — learned perceptual objects
Too broad for first work unless simpler levels fundamentally fail.

The campaign must decide where the first organism should begin.

# 5. Memory and "world model"

R3's checked absence is valuable, but symbolic `objectBelief{id,lastKnownLocation}` may be one particular ontology, not neutral memory.

Questions:

- Does the actor need object-centric memory immediately?
- Could episodic sensorimotor traces + local landmarks be enough?
- Should uncertainty/staleness be explicit or emerge from recency/confidence?
- Does a metric map overfit navigation?
- Can actor-private spatial memory be relational instead of global-coordinate?
- Which memory is necessary for history-dependent behaviour but not a hidden scene graph?

Candidate memory families:

### M0 — recency/history buffer
Raw local percept/action/outcome history.

### M1 — relational landmark memory
Encountered local relations and checked absence.

### M2 — actor-private metric/topological map
Built only from legal sensing/self-motion.

### M3 — predictive latent state
Learned compact state, initially shadow-only.

Do not assume M1 merely because R3 used it successfully.

# 6. Self vs world / sensorimotor contingencies

This is now a primary research axis, not a decorative shadow probe.

Core question:

> Can the organism discover or exploit regularities linking its own action to sensory/body consequences, and use mismatch to detect external/unmodelled change?

Candidate progression:

1. analytic short-horizon self-motion predictor;
2. learned local forward model;
3. context-conditioned sensorimotor contingency model;
4. shadow comparison across Owner/world perturbations.

Required controls:
- self-generated motion;
- matched external shove;
- world/object movement while actor is still;
- same visual displacement produced by self-motion vs object-motion;
- altered body dynamics;
- altered sensory mapping.

Do not turn prediction error into global reward by default.

# 7. Concern, motivation and normativity

The authored maintenance concern may be the largest hidden assumption in yesterday's O0.

We need to distinguish at least four very different things:

### C0 — externally authored task
"keep material at site".

Useful engineering scaffold; weakest endogenous normativity.

### C1 — body viability/homeostasis
internal variables make some trajectories materially better/worse for continued functioning.

Risk:
- collapse into safe inactivity;
- arbitrary global scalar;
- over-biologizing the experiment.

### C2 — competence / controllability
organism tends toward situations where it can produce/learn reliable effects.

Risk:
- curiosity/empowerment theatre;
- noisy-TV / attractor artifacts.

### C3 — habit / sensorimotor identity
ongoing patterns become self-maintaining local organizations; disruption creates pressure to restore/reorganize.

Potentially closer to an organism without requiring fake metabolism.

### C4 — plural motives
small set of heterogeneous concerns whose conflicts create context-sensitive meaning.

Risk:
- scheduler scorecard reappears.

The campaign must not choose "maintenance task" merely because it is easy to test.

# 8. Local Brain architecture — keep alternatives alive

Yesterday's layered decomposition:
`motor -> local spatial competence -> continuation/recovery -> later ReflexBrain`
is plausible but not yet earned.

Compare:

### L0 — hierarchical competence stack
clear interfaces, easy instrumentation, risk of central task-bot.

### L1 — Brooks-like behavior layers
parallel complete activity producers coupled to world; risk of arbitration complexity.

### L2 — predictive sensorimotor controller
actions organized around learned/analytic contingencies and prediction residuals.

### L3 — dynamical/habit system
persistent attractors/habits that interact with material constraints; risk of hidden limit cycles.

### L4 — hybrid
simple embodied competencies + persistent concern/habit + predictive self/world model.

No architecture wins by elegance.

A candidate should be judged by:
- scene reconfiguration;
- perturbation recovery;
- history dependence;
- transparency;
- failure quality;
- ability to create later semantic pressure.

# 9. One actor vs independent causal others

Yesterday O0 froze one cognitive actor to keep analysis clean.

Re-open this.

Hypotheses:

### A0 — one actor is sufficient
independent environment processes create enough pressure.

### A1 — one actor + non-cognitive moving processes
adds exogenous causality without social complexity.

### A2 — two simple autonomous actors
independent trajectories create qualitatively richer actor-relative meaning.

Potential value of A2:
- occlusion and inference about another body;
- competition/cooperation without scripted Owner pressure;
- same event can matter differently;
- social reafference/exafference boundary;
- world continues while Owner observes elsewhere.

Risk:
- doubles ambiguity and makes failure attribution harder.

Do not add a second actor merely for spectacle.

# 10. Owner role

Owner interaction must be decomposed.

Candidate modes:

1. observer only;
2. world manipulator;
3. body perturbation;
4. temporary body takeover;
5. communication;
6. authoring/rearranging environment between runs.

These are not equivalent.

For each mode ask:
- what causal evidence reaches actor?
- what does Owner know?
- does intervention create meaningful pressure or merely test hooks?
- can the actor continue when Owner disappears?

Owner should not have to manufacture every interesting event.

# 11. Research microscope without microscope gravity

Required distinction:

### organism surface
what can be watched/played without diagnostic knowledge;

### causal microscope
research-only trace, replay, branches, first divergence;

### qualification harness
frozen tests for a specific claim.

Rules:

- organism surface must be interesting enough to justify microscope use;
- every major instrumentation addition must answer a named uncertainty;
- diagnostic richness is not organism progress;
- no "machine life score";
- exploratory observation may precede frozen testing;
- freeze only after the phenomenon is understood enough to state a falsifier.
