## Live research truth

The project remains ReflexBrain Lab. Its long-term question is still whether a cheap learned actor-private semantic/reflex organ can add useful actor-relative meaning inside an already-living actor without becoming World authority, memory, planner or motor controller.

This reclassifies E0.0 as a **qualified body/microscope substrate donor**, not as a living-organism milestone.

**E0_BODY_SUBSTRATE_QUALIFIED / E0_ORGANISM_PROBE_FAIL**
