# 2. Evidence classes used by the control room

Every imported finding must be classified.

## CORE

Part of current Owner-level project vision or a defended project-wide invariant.

## EVIDENCE

A narrow claim supported by an actual bounded experiment and appropriate source/CI evidence.

## DONOR

Mechanism/finding worth reusing or re-testing, but not canonical ReflexBrain architecture.

## SCOUT

Forward-looking exploration that may be ahead of current foundation qualification.

## FAILED

A negative result that materially constrains future work.

## OBSOLETE

Historical route that should not continue by inertia.

## OPEN

Important question without enough evidence.

Machine PASS never overrides Owner product/experiential FAIL.

# 6. Cross-lab synthesis: what changed in our understanding

The strongest new combined pattern is not:

> better perception -> better intelligence.

It is:

> **competence and significance are relational, historical and domain-bounded.**

Evidence now points toward several distinct transitions:

### Transition A — physical possibility

Can this body actually do something here?

Combat Lab donor territory.

### Transition B — private evidence

What did this actor legally sense about what just happened?

Canonical ReflexBrain / P02 / C01 territory.

### Transition C — historical relation

Does this current evidence connect to something already belonging to this actor?

LLM NPC #156/#158 territory.

### Transition D — local consequence / continuation

Can local machinery continue or resume useful action without higher cognition?

Combat / R6 local runtime / Living Organism donor territory.

### Transition E — competence failure / uncertainty

Does the actor's current local model cease to predict/control the situation?

Living Organism scout territory; contact-domain counterexamples are especially relevant.

### Transition F — reason formation / escalation

Does the situation merely differ, or does it create a genuinely actor-owned unresolved question/matter worth local or higher cognition?

LLM NPC #159/#160 expose this as a major missing layer.

This sequence should be investigated.

It must not be hard-coded as final architecture.

# 7. Current ReflexBrain competence frontier

The control room identifies five high-value frontier questions.

## F1 — actor-relative relevance from history, not event labels

Question:

> Can the same legal private evidence become behaviorally/semantically relevant only when it intersects an actor-owned historical relation, without importing a pre-labelled "matter" ontology?

Motivation:
- #156 and #158 show strong relational relevance donors;
- canonical ReflexBrain wants learned actor-relative meaning rather than host-authored relevance.

Risk:
- simply cloning R6 `Matter`/commitment objects would import the answer.

Status:
**HIGH PRIORITY OPEN.**

---

## F2 — local competence applicability / failure detection

Question:

> Can a local learned model know when its own predictions/actions are leaving the domain in which they are reliable?

Motivation:
- Living Organism free-motion model can succeed strongly in free motion and fail badly at contact;
- Combat shows morphology/contact/material conditions change what actions mean physically.

Potential significance:
- competence failure itself may provide natural actor-relative semantic pressure.

Status:
**HIGH PRIORITY OPEN / SCOUT-DERIVED.**

---

## F3 — owned-reason formation

Question:

> What makes a private event become a genuinely actor-owned reason to reconsider or act, rather than merely another percept?

Motivation:
- #159 shows encounter density alone produces no owned reasons;
- #160 shows time can matter only through an already-owned commitment;
- current systems mostly require some concern/matter to exist first.

This is one of the biggest missing bridges between local life and higher cognition.

Status:
**VERY HIGH PRIORITY OPEN.**

---

## F4 — active information acquisition

Question:

> When current private evidence is insufficient, can the local organism learn that an information-seeking action is useful without the researcher naming "uncertainty" or scripting CHECK?

Motivation:
- canonical C01 has authored CHECK;
- Living Organism explored active visual inspection;
- future embodied intelligence should be able to move/gaze/test to resolve uncertainty.

Status:
**HIGH PRIORITY OPEN.**

---

## F5 — local-to-higher cognition escalation

Question:

> Can local cognition recognize that its current competence/evidence is insufficient and escalate a compact unresolved problem to slower/higher cognition without turning the LLM into the organism heartbeat?

Motivation:
- LLM Live NPC shows perception alone is cheap but reasons/actions do not self-originate;
- provider capability must match actual World/runtime capabilities;
- too much higher cognition can reduce executable behavior when local action substrate is weak;
- Owner wants architecture flexible across local brain / Jev / mini-LLM / Luna/Sol rather than frozen hierarchy.

Status:
**HIGH PRIORITY OPEN, BUT DEPENDS ON F2/F3.**

2. **CLASSIFY**
   - CORE / EVIDENCE / DONOR / SCOUT / FAILED / OBSOLETE / OPEN;

3. **RECONCILE**
   - compare new findings against canonical causal boundaries;

4. **EXTRACT**
   - import questions and donor mechanisms, not whole architectures by default;

5. **FALSIFY**
   - design ReflexBrain-native experiments for the highest-value unresolved competence;

6. **PROMOTE**
   - only after bounded evidence and Owner judgement;

   - stop labs/features that add machinery without new competence.
