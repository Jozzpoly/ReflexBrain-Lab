# Pre-O0 Evaluation, Owner Surface and Microscope Deep Dive — 2026-10-06

Status: **FOUNDATIONAL RESEARCH-METHOD AUDIT · NO "LIFE SCORE"**

## Core problem

ReflexBrain Lab repeatedly improved its instrumentation faster than its organism.

A research system can become excellent at:
- replay;
- provenance;
- counterfactuals;
- benchmark scores;
- deterministic checks;

while the object under study remains poor.

Therefore evaluation must prevent **microscope gravity**.

---

# 1. Four different surfaces

## A. organism surface

What exists if all debug UI is hidden.

Owner/researcher can:
- watch;
- interact through World-legal means;
- observe body/world behavior.

This is the closest thing to product/phenomenon reality.

## B. causal microscope

Research-only:
- World truth;
- private sensory evidence;
- private internal state/history;
- motor demand;
- body outcome;
- first divergence;
- intervention provenance.

Purpose:
explain why a phenomenon happened.

It does not define whether the phenomenon is valuable.

## C. qualification harness

Frozen scenario/probe for one specific claim.

Examples:
- hidden relocation does not leak;
- self-generated vs external displacement;
- checked absence;
- same policy across rearranged scenes.

Purpose:
falsify a scoped mechanism.

It must not become the organism's curriculum by default.

## D. Owner observation/evaluation

Owner judges experiential/product-level phenomena:
- trajectory ownership;
- watchability;
- coherence;
- body feel;
- whether interactions feel systemic or demo-like;
- whether there is something worth pursuing.

Owner is not oracle for internal mechanism correctness.

These planes must remain distinct.

---

# 2. "Watchability" as a research signal

Persistent Playground produced a qualitative signal:
Owner wanted to keep watching/interacting despite obvious technical poverty.

This is not scientific proof of life.

But it is valuable product/research evidence.

Possible components:

- activity continues without prompting;
- behavior has enough continuity to form expectations;
- behavior is not fully predictable;
- perturbations have persistent consequences;
- organism seems to own an unfinished trajectory;
- world has off-screen or independent causal activity;
- visible behavior can be related back to understandable causes.

Call this **watchability**, not "alive score".

Never optimize directly for watchability as a scalar.

Use it as Owner-sensitive evidence that a research direction exposes a worthwhile phenomenon.

---

# 3. Avoid performative life

A system can look alive because of:
- idle animation;
- random motor noise;
- smooth easing;
- head/eye motion;
- social animation;
- UI labels;
- unpredictability;
- multiple moving entities.

These may improve presentation without improving organism structure.

Therefore Owner surface should support **causal perturbation**:
Owner can alter World conditions and see whether behavior reorganizes coherently.

A good-looking idle loop that ignores causal changes should fail quickly.

---

# 4. Blindness and priming

E0 Owner-view title leak showed even small UI text can prime interpretation.

Guidelines:

### early exploratory viewing
Do not over-blind.
Owner should understand controls and basic world vocabulary.

### mechanism comparison
Hide labels that reveal expected condition/result.

### experiential judgement
Avoid diagnostic words such as:
- alive;
- adaptive;
- learned;
- heavy;
- important;
- target;
unless they are directly perceptible game concepts.

### post-run reveal
Allow microscope after natural observation.

Do not turn every Owner session into psychophysics.

---

# 5. Evidence planes

Keep at least:

## P0 — implementation correctness
types, deterministic invariants, no crashes.

## P1 — physical/causal mechanism
contact, occlusion, private-state provenance, replay.

## P2 — organism competence
zero-input trajectory, recovery, history dependence, scene transfer.

## P3 — learning/adaptation
held-out causal generalization, recalibration, skill acquisition.

## P4 — semantic ReflexBrain competence
learned actor-relative relation with real downstream consumer.

## P5 — Owner experiential/product evidence
something worth watching/playing; convincing continuity; useful feel.

No lower plane promotes higher plane automatically.

---

# 6. Organism signatures matrix

Do not reduce to one metric.

Potential signatures:

| signature | machine question | Owner question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| trajectory ownership | does state/action continue without Owner and depend on private history? | does it seem to have unfinished business of its own? |
| material embodiment | do motor demands meet body/world constraints? | does motion/contact feel rule-governed? |
| private ignorance | does hidden change remain hidden? | does search/uncertainty look believable rather than omniscient? |
| history dependence | can matched current observations diverge from relevant history? | do earlier experiences visibly matter later? |
| self/world distinction | can matched self/external changes produce different private evidence? | do external perturbations produce coherent surprise/recovery? |
| adaptive recovery | does failure evidence change later local action without scripts? | does it stop looking stuck or randomly reset? |
| ecology autonomy | do independent processes alter world without Owner? | is there something to return to after looking away? |
| generality | same mechanisms survive rearrangement | does it stop feeling like a prepared demo? |

No row alone is sufficient.

# 7. First-divergence methodology

Retain from R3/Companion-like work.

For paired runs, compare planes:

1. World divergence;
2. sensory evidence divergence;
3. private memory/internal-state divergence;
4. local action organization divergence;
5. motor demand divergence;
6. physical outcome divergence.

Interpretation depends on intervention.

Example:
hidden World relocation may cause World divergence before actor evidence.
Actor decision should not diverge before private evidence.

This is one of the strongest reusable research instruments.

---

# 8. Counterfactual scope discipline

A replay branch answers only controlled channels.

Do not claim:
"all other causes are equal"
unless the experiment controls them.

Declare:
- deterministic seed/state;
- controlled intervention;
- observed channels;
- unknown/uninstrumented channels.

Counterfactuals find differences.
They do not prove metaphysical equivalence.

---

# 9. Exploratory vs qualification mode

## exploratory
Purpose:
discover phenomenon.

Allowed:
- inspect;
- tweak;
- play;
- observe weirdness;
- form hypotheses.

Claims:
tentative.

## qualification
Purpose:
test named hypothesis.

Requires:
- frozen mechanism;
- frozen scenario family;
- predeclared falsifier;
- held-out/perturbation controls where relevant.

Do not freeze too early.

Yesterday O0 was frozen before foundations were sufficiently understood.

This campaign corrects that.

---

# 10. Failure taxonomy

Every FAIL should be classified if possible:

### phenomenon failure
the organism simply does not show intended property.

### mechanism failure
candidate mechanism does not produce phenomenon.

### ecology failure
world does not create necessary pressure.

### perception failure
legal evidence is insufficient/too rich/wrongly structured.

### body failure
effectivity/motion prevents meaningful interaction.

### adaptation failure
history updates wrong layer or overfits.

### instrumentation failure
trace/probe cannot support claim.

### experiential failure
machine mechanisms exist but Owner surface remains worthless/unconvincing.

This prevents fixing the wrong layer.

---

# 11. Anti-benchmark rules

- do not add scenario solely because it repairs one FAIL;
- do not train on held-out falsifier;
- do not count fixture variants as generalization if policy sees their structure;
- do not promote one score because it correlates with intended phenomenon;
- periodically create Owner/random legal rearrangements unknown to test author;
- require qualitative new pressure before capacity increases.

---

# 12. Research debt budget

Instrumentation is not free.

For every significant microscope feature, ask:

1. Which current uncertainty does it resolve?
2. Could a simpler trace answer it?
3. Will organism code consume it? If yes, stop: it may be leaking.
4. Does it materially reduce future ambiguity?

If no clear answer, do not build it.

This is a direct response to R2/R3 apparatus growth.

---

# 13. Owner burden

Owner should not need to:
- click through scripted test cases;
- manually create every pressure event;
- inspect JSON;
- validate internal variables;
- run long deterministic campaigns.

Owner should primarily:
- watch;
- perturb naturally;
- report feel/observations;
- make high-level judgments.

Machine harness and agent should handle mechanistic qualification.

This keeps Owner attention focused on the evidence plane only Owner can provide.

---

# 14. Future review cadence

A candidate organism should face:

### Gate A — machine substrate
no Owner needed.

### Gate B — agent exploratory observation
research agent plays/watches and hunts obvious artifacts.

### Gate C — Owner natural contact
minimal priming.

### Gate D — mechanistic follow-up
use microscope to explain Owner observations.

### Gate E — revision or promotion
only after both experiential and mechanism planes align.

This is better than:
machine PASS -> Owner demo -> declare milestone.

---

# Current conclusion

The campaign should preserve powerful causal instruments but aggressively prevent them from becoming the project.

Success criterion for pre-O0 research is not:
"we have enough tests."

It is:

> we understand the organism question well enough that the first implementation exposes a phenomenon worth testing, and our instruments can explain it without defining it.
