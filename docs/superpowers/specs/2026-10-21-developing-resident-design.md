# Developing Resident — Design Candidate, NOT Final Architecture
**2026-10-21 · Owner-approved direction, written design pending Owner review · ReflexBrain Lab**
**Status:** DESIGN FOR REVIEW. **No implementation of the new resident is authorized by this document alone.** Existing E0/B0, B1, M1, S0, OL0, P0/P1 and D0/D1 sources remain donor evidence rather than canonical learned/living systems.

## Decision in 60 seconds — Owner-readable layer

**Direction:** one continuing physically embodied resident on an existing, explicitly selected runnable donor; learn actor-private action–consequence regularities in normal life, check whether those actually change later physical action against very cheap baselines; preserve a clear return route to **ReflexBrain proper** as soon as genuinely ambiguous actor-relative event significance has a real consumer. This is **not** the old cyan `Occupant` with a bigger map.

**Current evidence:** E0/D1, optional B1 and scoped #80 observation/checkpoint fixes are executable; **no developing resident or learned semantic competence is qualified**. B1's moved disk is a researcher-world effect, not yet a demonstrated actor-private understood outcome.

**Three vetoes:** (1) no legal evidence that an effect can be observed → do not train on hidden ground truth; (2) simple reactive/action-tape control matches the alleged new competence → do not promote learned skill; (3) recurrent natural relevance questions appear → stop polishing host and investigate the semantic consumer.

**Execution architecture status:** THIS SPEC IS A REVIEWABLE CANDIDATE, not permission to merge or implement a new subsystem; the scoped G0 research repair #80 has independently passed actual CI. The later plan must specify an explicitly selected source branch and individually chosen donors.

## 2. Three serious routes — and the provisional choice

### A. Repair and increment the four-mode `Occupant`
**Pros:** cheapest and reuses all current tests/UI. **Cons:** cements authored turquoise attraction, programmed yield/inspection and short last-bearing memory as the agent's reason to exist. A more sophisticated controller could still be an elegant color-chaser. **Disposition:** retain only as a diagnostic negative baseline; do not silently evolve it into the target resident.

### B. Developmental single resident on the existing physical donor (preferred first architectural experiment)
One organism persists. An explicitly *authored but simple* developmental activity source permits sensorimotor attempts; a checkpointed learner acquires an actor-private regularity of action → observed body/material consequence; at first it makes **shadow predictions only**; after earned controls it may affect the next action/probe selection. The World continues independently and Owner interventions do not reset the brain. **Pros:** fastest path to testing *whether experience changes later actor activity*, makes previous failure modes measurable. **Cons:** learning-progress/novelty can generate walls, noise addiction, useless poking and a counterfeit "curious" personality; a tiny environment may artificially shape the winner. **Disposition:** design one measured developmental challenger plus cheaper controls, do not promise a full organism from it.

### C. Endogenous homeostatic or habitual organism from the start
One or more body-grounded viability/habit variables create persistent self-maintenance pressure. **Pros:** possible genuine actor-relative relevance. **Cons:** idle safe attractor, fake energy economy, single-resource shuttle, handcrafted thresholds, enormous tuning/physics confounds. **Disposition:** keep as a distinct falsifiable challenger, not a required prerequisite of the first implementation; revisit if B generates procedural competence but no worthwhile sustained activity.

Hybridizing B+C before they have separate evidence hides causal contributions. A two-NPC social ecology, full 3D body, cloud simulation cluster, GPU-scale agent, perfect map and learned general semantic model are explicitly outside the first implementation.

## 3. First physical experiential substrate, not a manufactured task
**One world, one resident** on entry. Use the already executable E0 `LivingWorld` and opt-in B0/B1 morphology donor, not a new physics engine or gameplay monolith. Build a sufficiently open and permissive test ecology from **ordinary physical interactions**: traversable alternatives, at least two movable bodies with different spatial constraints, walls/corridors as part of varied space (not a mandatory gate puzzle), and at least one independently moving real object/process. Matter remains consequential after the actor leaves. No callback `completedGoal()`, no object `affordance=true` field consumed by the actor.

The tiny, paired B0/B1 corridor display is a **body-comparison research microscope**, not the default habitat or public living organism. The first true experimental occupant should have one primary viewport, actual matter, native 96RGB private strip, ability to intervene manually, pause, fork/restore, resume without resetting actor history, and visible action/experience state distinct from omniscient researcher state. The minimal primary room must be judged by actual play/observation; dimensions will not be frozen on paper.

**Body choice is not a dogma.** B0 can push; B1 offers orientation-sensitive constraints; B1 also changes rotational inertia and may snag. Select a body for the first integrated test based on achieved physical repertoires and short exploratory behavior, not just the 1.7-wide manufactured corridor win. A second morphology remains a falsifier of body-specific learning.

## 4. Causal truth, sensing and action credit
- World truth is authoritative only for physics and research afterstate, never for actor decisions. Actor inputs derive only from a declared lawful private sensorium: current and prior 30Hz 96RGB, contact, proprioception, own allowed motor-feedback/intention data, internal memory. No precise World x/y, collider handles, colors tagged as named objects, hidden movement phase, dock distance, reward verdict or future states.
- Keep explicit **observation time vs World time**. A host edit while paused changes physical World but does not retroactively create actor sensation at that tick. The view distinguishes last-delivered private frame from current host geometry until the next scheduled lawful sample.
- Motor intent, requested actuation, pre-transducer actuator readback (if added), physical aftereffect and unmeasured external influence are separate. A host-owned provenance exclusion can protect offline training sets but must never be described as actor's learned inference. Actor-side inference must be honest about unidentifiable cases; `UNKNOWN` is a permitted outcome.
- Snapshot/replay must include actual physics, chosen body morphology, actor's learned parameters, private episodic/sensor state, actuator / held controls and declared clock. Old untagged version-3 snapshots are B0. Never let restore silently swap a B0 collider under a B1 label.
- Distinguish host-visible feedback from actor-legible consequences. A researcher can truthfully measure that a box moved; the actor may not yet be able to infer which box moved, how, or why.

## 5. The learned organ for the first experiment (tentative role, NOT locked model family)
A small, checkpointed **sensorimotor action–consequence hypothesis** is preferable to a scene classifier or a global semantic labeler. It consumes bounded preceding private context, actual candidate actuator feedback (if legal), and consequent private sensor/body change. It should learn at least one *conditional* physical capability: e.g., when attempted forward action produces continued displacement vs stalls at contact, or when orientation changes whether a material passage becomes reachable. These are examples for stress-testing, not hardcoded target classes or a promised specific competence.

At first, run the learned hypothesis in **shadow mode** against the unchanged genuine actuator history. Challenge a cheap no-memory physical rule, fixed body-dynamics prior, short-proprio history, randomized probe and equivalent-information comparator. Improvement in passive prediction is **not** enough. Its eventual behavioral influence must be causally isolated: if it predicts a useful response, an executive can select a bounded probe/action, while physical consequences remain Rapier's authority.

Avoid ranking models by internal loss alone; OL0 demonstrated that a lower forecast error can make a worse online controller. A new neural network is not required by the initial milestone; compact per-context online statistics are admissible challengers, provided learning and learned state are distinguished from authored rule tables. The learning state must survive a meaningful time horizon and checkpoint. Local compute first, train on RTX A4500 only once a well-defined physical error and validated benefit exist.

## 6. The biggest risk: what sustains the resident's own activity?
Initial developmental pressure is an explicitly designed experimental source of activity, **not proof of endogenous life**. It should occasionally provoke lawful action-outcome probes and also allow cessation, reacquisition and a switch of activity when local attempts are uninformative or harmful. Its exact rule, schedule and biases must be exposed to the research observer as authored.

Do not use cyan affinity, total travel, novelty itself, raw prediction error, contact count, resource visits, time alive, test oracle distance or hidden actor world-knowledge as a universal purpose. No extra reward just to make the resident look interested. Comparators must include an always-probe version, an independent randomized/cheap reactive version, and World-only.

There are two success questions, not one:
- **Developmental competence:** did legally acquired private history make later action selection and physical consequence measurably different **beyond** a trivial rule, and does that survive scene/material/body perturbation?
- **Continuing resident quality:** when Owner stops touching the world, is there coherent, revisable, physically consequential activity worth observing? This is a higher, independent, Owner-sensitive judgement. One narrow competence PASS does not cancel ordinary-life FAIL.

## 7. Multi-tier validation as part of the artifact, not a gigantic testing bureaucracy

### G0 — truth and instrumentation
Bounded #80 repairs and red→green native + Chrome tests: shape-safe restore, unchanged 30Hz sampling semantics after paused edits, honest stale observation label, full backup/fork continuity, no private host data. Fail or unknown blocks any causal learning claim.

### G1 — *possibility* AND private observability of consequential physical activity
Headless plus short live browser inspection of material configurations using actual candidate body/motor: can lawful actions cause distinct persistent results, **and does the actor's legal temporally integrated RGB/touch/proprio/action-copy evidence allow that consequence to be inferred at all?** The real object x/y displacement and collision handles are researcher-only afterstate, never learning labels. Include optic-flow/parallax vantage changes and unchanged visual-contact counterexamples; allow `UNKNOWN` when two physical outcomes are privately aliased. A preselected corridor win does not qualify a rich habitat. Record body snagging, recovery, terminal boredom and real Owner feedback; change substrate only when there is a demonstrated missing physical or epistemic affordance.

### G2 — developmental actor baseline
Introduce **one** replacement resident, not an addon to turquoise `Occupant`: explicit minimal activity source; bounded legal sensorimotor memory; checkpoint; zero-Owner continuation. A limited authored control resident may be deliberately boring; a richer developmental challenger is compared, not smuggled into its policy. Distinguish *O-CTRL host sufficiency* from *O-DEV development* rather than forcing O-CTRL to pass an implicit self-direction test before the learner exists. At this stage **no learner promotion** and no new semantic claims.

### G3 — online hypothesis, shadow first
Freeze comparison arrangements *before observing the test outcome*. Use source-independent training/world perturbation and held-out arrangements; assess conditional predictions of aftereffects vs fixed, cheap and equal-information controls. If a private history is insufficient to distinguish real cases, report an identifiability/observability boundary instead of constructing target leaks or making a larger model guess.

### G4 — causal effect on later actor action
At the **same true physical checkpoint and declared actuator/history intervention boundary**, compare learned parameters with untrained/ablated/shuffled alternatives. Require the same legitimate sensory/motor prefix **until the first policy divergence**; afterwards the world and future private frames may diverge naturally. Do not call two post-divergence experiences 'matched'; record the first cause and evaluate later consequences under distinct real physics. Also compare recorded action-replay and cheap reactive/predictive rules. Require that altered decisions cause a **physically meaningful, later observable difference** in at least one genuinely new pressure arrangement — not just movement statistics. A strong result needs repeatability, but exact sample counts/thresholds are set in a separately frozen experiment protocol before running, not invented retroactively to pass.

### G5 — Owner play/observe/revise
Only if there is a new material/behavioral capacity worth human attention: one accessible, working living world on a **verified** test URL or local runnable artifact, with mobile input if promised, checkpoint + intervention and clear provenance. The Owner's observed FAIL is not rewritten by CI. Reopen world/body/brain decisions when the experience is poor. No silent publishing or merging.

### G6 — widen before scaling
Try changed body inertia/friction, independently acting matter, altered corridor/object geometry, clutter, material history washout, external versus self action, and ordinary unobserved intervals. Only durable local efficacy plus Owner value motivates bigger sensors, training time, GPU or wider habitat.

## 8. Adversarial hypotheses designed to embarrass us
1. **Action-sequence shortcut:** an open-loop action tape reproduces 'learned' outcomes because scene seeds and geometry are frozen.
2. **Nuisance predictor:** the model learns rail phase/color artifacts, not contact/body contingency; fail after natural geometry/material variation.
3. **Unidentifiable author:** manual takeover produces identical private original frames to self-issued motor, so apparent self/source classification comes from host-only gate.
4. **Prediction ≠ control:** shadow forecast improves while physical work or later actionable options decrease.
5. **Noisy-TV / wall obsession:** learned policy maximizes surprise near unpredictable sweeper or continuous wall impact.
6. **Task-bot disguise:** apparent material intelligence entirely explained by authored turquoise/dock prior, hidden World IDs or special goal success trigger.
7. **Morphed physics mismatch:** B1 declared body is actually restored legacy B0, or controller's learned dynamics are not reset under a real body change.
8. **Snapshot contamination:** a learned behavior difference comes from different physics RNG, fixed nonidentical history or sensory cache rather than private history ablation.
9. **Owner-performance mirage:** laboratory controls pass but ordinary-play observer still sees a small boring cage and a malfunctioning resident.
10. **Infrastructure trap:** weeks of instrumentation/physics refinements pass without a single new actor behavior or testable meaningful competence. Pause substrate growth unless the next change directly enables a previously impossible actor/world interaction.

For each future positive claim, identify which of these was actively challenged and which remains untested.

These two frontiers cannot be collapsed into one sign of 'brain success'. Stage B / O-DEV's sensorimotor learner improves action-effect competence; it is not automatically a learned actor-relative significance organ. **Hard early EXIT/VETO:** as soon as one ordinary live host generates **recurring actor-relative ambiguous consequences** that (H7) are not resolved by a cheap factual/local rule, (H8) genuinely affect an ongoing activity, and (H9) recur across surface variation, return to a *scoped ReflexBrain proper* causal-consumer/shadow research line **without waiting for a beautiful mature organism or full O-DEV**. Do not keep optimizing B1, maps, graphics, memory, brain size or the world merely because their defects remain visible. H1–H6 host sufficiency must remain adequate; if movement defects dominate observed results, improve only that blocker. This is a **branch in execution**, not an additional gate after G6.

**Private supervision does not follow automatically from material physics:** `LivingWorld.observe()` gives 96 colors (no distance, object ID, or object displacement label), touch impulse sectors and body-relative velocity/rotation. `PrivateVision` currently triangulates a uniquely visible turquoise patch under an explicitly **stationary-fragment** assumption and is not consumed by the old `Occupant`. A pushed moving fragment violates that assumption; A1 already showed a contact can preserve approximately identical relative RGB while radically changing absolute material progress. Hence B1's verified 0.976m displacement **does not qualify** the actor as having witnessed, understood or learned that displacement. The proposed first action-effect learner MUST specify a trace of lawful available input *before* selecting a supervision target. Predictions are only about future legal private observations or appropriately declared self/bodily signals; researcher World outcome is an **evaluation variable only**, never direct training truth. No private identifiability → no learner success regardless of World displacement.

**A sample's experimental provenance is not an agent sense:** P0's host-owned exclusion of manual takeover can keep a researcher training corpus clean, but it cannot be used as evidence the actor inferred its own action authorship. A body readback (P1) may be a lawful new sensor if specified; a manual/Owner semantic flag is a research label and must stay out of inference. Same legal trace may have multiple causal origins; preserve uncertainty rather than train impossible binary labels.

**Original O-DEV falsifiers are prerequisites, not inventions here:** OD1 wall-clinging, OD2 unlearnable noisy process, OD3 trivial process saturation, OD4 representation partition perturbation, OD5 ephemeral track-token leak and OD6 ecology-only ablation. It is possible to create apparent learning progress merely by changing a feature partition or token name; these variants must be predeclared before claiming endogenous interest. Do not optimize 'learning progress' as if it were already actor-relative meaning.

**The meaning/value gap is independent of contact competence:** even if a learner learns when it can push a disk or fit through a gap, that does not explain *why the actor pursues a particular situation*. A research-scaffold developmental probe policy can be declared and tested, but its authored choice of which local hypotheses are worth probing is not emergent value. Do not rename a cleverly scheduled probe library 'self-originated curiosity'. Homeostatic and habit alternatives stay distinct falsifiers, not decorative flavors of one monolithic novelty reward.

**Causal test must pay a trivial-control tax:** same World afterstate alone is not enough; the first difference between full and memory-ablated policy should be attributable to private learning state, and its physical aftereffect must beat (or demonstrate a different useful ability than) motor tapes, cheap reactive body-stall rules, bounded gaze/turn exploration and matched-information memory. Time spent looking, moved distance, RGB novelty and number of contacts are **secondary microscopy only**. A result failing these controls is valuable negative evidence, not a reason to add more layers and retune the scene.

**Deliverable discipline:** use this candidate only as design review material. Convert it to a precise implementation plan after written-spec review, then implement small experimentally meaningful increments on isolated branches. The recent #80 contract fix is the separately approved bounded work and does not approve the architecture. Favor an Owner-visible small coherent living experience early *when a new actual behavior exists*, then revise via honest qualitative feedback. Keep a single existing canonical research router and link this spec; avoid another competing 'current' document.
