# Pre-O0 Project Boundary and Exit Condition — 2026-10-06

Status: **SCOPE SAFETY · PREVENT ORGANISM-SUBSTRATE TAKEOVER OF REFLEXBRAIN LAB**

## Core risk

The project correctly discovered that ReflexBrain cannot be studied honestly inside a fake/empty actor.

But this can invert into a new failure:

> building the "perfect organism" becomes a prerequisite for ever returning to ReflexBrain.

That would turn ReflexBrain Lab into:
- Local Brain Lab;
- embodied-agent platform;
- world simulation project;
- developmental robotics project;
- miniature Feniks/SPC.

This is not the intended scope.

---

# 1. Original/durable ReflexBrain purpose

The durable question remains:

> Can a cheap local learned mechanism extract useful actor-relative meaning from temporal private experience and improve an already-living actor without becoming World authority, factual memory, whole planner or motor controller?

The substrate campaign exists only because previous hosts were too causally poor or fake for this question to be meaningful.

Therefore:

**organism foundation is a prerequisite investigation, not the final product of ReflexBrain Lab.**

---

# 2. Minimum sufficient host, not complete organism

The host becomes sufficient when it produces **real residual semantic pressure**.

It does not need:
- complete autonomy;
- human-like motivation;
- rich social cognition;
- general planning;
- perfect perception;
- open-ended development;
- realistic metabolism;
- production-quality game world.

It needs enough structure that a repeated class of problems remains after ordinary competence.

---

# 3. Host sufficiency signatures

A host is likely sufficient for ReflexBrain insertion when most are true:

### H1 — actor-owned temporal trajectory
There is something ongoing before a candidate semantic event occurs.

### H2 — private history matters
The same current percept can legitimately mean something different because histories differ.

### H3 — partial knowledge
World truth and actor knowledge diverge naturally.

### H4 — material consequence
Different interpretations/actions can eventually create different body/world outcomes.

### H5 — independent causal pressure
Events occur without Owner/test harness manufacturing them.

### H6 — ordinary Local Brain competence
Basic movement/checking/recovery works well enough that failures are not dominated by locomotion bugs.

### H7 — ambiguous actor-relative event
At least one recurring situation cannot be resolved by factual lookup or trivial local rule.

### H8 — real downstream consumer
Different interpretation would affect a continuing activity, investigation, interruption, communication, or escalation.

### H9 — semantic generalization opportunity
The relevant distinction recurs across surface variation and cannot be reduced to one object ID/layout/timer.

When H7–H9 appear naturally, ReflexBrain research should resume even if the host remains imperfect.

---

# 4. "Residual semantic pressure" definition

A candidate residual pressure is **not**:

- obstacle avoidance;
- remembering object location;
- calibrating turn rate;
- detecting contact;
- knowing that a route is blocked;
- object tracking;
- executing a known carry interaction;
- pure path planning.

Those are ordinary factual/sensorimotor competence.

A stronger semantic pressure has form:

> given legal private history and current situation, what does this event/relation mean **for this actor's ongoing life**, in a way that generalizes beyond exact fixture identity?

Examples later:

- Is this unexpected event relevant enough to abandon or preserve current activity?
- Does this communication refer to one of my unresolved concerns?
- Is a familiar-looking situation causally analogous to one that mattered before?
- Does unexplained change indicate a body/world issue worth investigation?
- Which of several ongoing concerns does this ambiguous event bear on?
- Is another actor's behavior incidental or consequential to my current trajectory?

Do not predefine these as output axes.

---

# 5. Exit condition from pre-O0 campaign

The campaign should end **before** all foundational questions are solved.

Exit when:

1. V0 boundaries are sufficiently scoped;
2. one substrate candidate maximizes information gain and remains replaceable;
3. the implementation can generate H1–H6 without major hidden shortcuts;
4. design plausibly creates at least one route toward H7–H9;
5. remaining uncertainty is better answered by a live organism than more paper analysis.

At that point:
**build.**

Not before.
Not much later.

---

# 6. Stop building host when semantic pressure appears

Once a live host repeatedly produces a qualified H7/H8 situation:

Do not keep polishing:
- map;
- body;
- perception;
- memory;

unless those are the cause of ambiguity/artifact.

Instead:
- freeze the host enough;
- characterize the pressure;
- build oracle/shadow semantic consumer;
- return to ReflexBrain.

This is the escape hatch from endless embodiment research.

---

# 7. Donor boundary

If a needed host subsystem grows into a general problem:

Examples:
- sophisticated navigation;
- object tracking;
- complex physics;
- social cognition;
- procedural world generation;

prefer:
- a bounded scaffold;
- a donor from another lab/project;
- a fake whose limitations are explicit;

rather than solving the entire domain inside ReflexBrain.

The fake is acceptable if it does **not** answer the ReflexBrain question for the actor.

Example:
a deterministic perceptual tracklet scaffold may be okay.
A semantic "this is the important object for your concern" scaffold is not.

---

# 8. Relation to Persistent Playground

Playground direction is valuable because it approached H1:
actor-owned temporal trajectory.

But it lacked enough:
- H2 private meaningful history;
- H3 rich partial knowledge;
- H6 robust ordinary competence;
- H7–H9 grounded semantic opportunity.

The goal is not to rebuild Playground until it is perfect.

The goal is to cross the **host sufficiency threshold** that Playground only approached.

---

# 9. Relation to R3

R3 strongly supported:
- H2/H3;
- H5;
- causal research tools.

But weak material body and fixture-shaped policies made H1/H4/H6 experiential quality poor.

A new host should recover those dimensions just enough.

Do not rebuild all R3 semantic campaigns before host sufficiency.

---

# 10. Relation to future SPC/Feniks

Long-term worlds may eventually supply a much richer host than ReflexBrain Lab can.

That is fine.

ReflexBrain Lab should discover transferable competence and invariants, not own the final world.

A successful ReflexBrain mechanism should later be transplantable into richer organisms.

---

# Current boundary statement

**Pre-O0 is a substrate sufficiency campaign, not an artificial-life project.**

Its success is:

> creating the smallest honest organism/world loop that naturally generates repeated actor-relative semantic questions worth learning.

Once that happens, the campaign should stop expanding and ReflexBrain proper should resume.
