# RB-ACT/P0 — actor-private action→observation model BEFORE epistemic agency
**FROZEN BEFORE EXECUTION, 2026-10-21. Research-only, stacked on PR #81 SHA 010531a. No new brain promotion or deployment.**

## Why this, not A1 again
A1 (#77) already showed: in a deliberately RGB-aliased pair, fixed pushing separates body response by lawful touch/proprio, and trivial speed threshold may suffice. O-DEV/V0 (#81) only scores own mobility and never uses its retina for actions; its private history changes later action but transfer is inconsistent, and randomness wins in the changed layout. Neither is proof of useful active knowledge gathering. Before implementing curiosity/planning, test whether a *small online learnt action-conditioned predictor of future lawful observations* gains practical information not already contained in simple actor-body rules.

## Fixed experiment and interventions
- Same Rapier 120Hz, private RGB/touch/proprio 30Hz, B0/B1. A harness proposes bounded, fixed 32-physics-tick probes chosen **without secret world information**, records consecutive lawful observations, and has a host-only microscope for material outcomes. There is no actor policy or chosen curiosity in P0. Use `LivingWorld(false, shape)` with multiple **physically different** loose-body, fixed-wall, occlusion and backstop configurations. Scene config/handle must never reach the learned model.
- Train a bounded local nonparametric predictor from actor-private `(before frame, executed motor)→(later forward/lateral/touch)` pairs. Predeclare descriptors: body forward/lateral/angular velocity, total touch, coarse front-retina lightness (color values, not object ID), and motor drive/turn. No exact world coordinate, color-ID, hidden wall flag, scenario name, outcome label or success oracle. Allow at most 384 retained real examples, bounded numeric distance. Choose a fixed nearest-neighbor interpolator with K=7 and no hyperparameter sweep.
- Compare to **(a)** zero-order persistence in proprio/touch, **(b)** globally trained action-conditioned mean with the same training examples, and **(c)** a fixed inexpensive own-body/contact reactive prediction rule. Compute held-out mean absolute prediction error, not just training loss. The cheap-rule form and all prediction components are set before seeing held-out results. No automatic PASS threshold favorable to the learner.
- Explicitly separate training layouts (free run, loose object, hard backstop, a light corridor) from withheld changed contexts (shifted backstop, larger loose body, alternate geometry), with B0/B1 reported separately. Keep full counts and contact density; if the test lacks actual evidence-rich frames, verdict **UNDERPOWERED**, not a model PASS.
- A stronger scientific claim is prohibited until predicted evidence changes a **future actor-chosen action** and materially beats equal-cost speed/touch-stall/turn, random and action-tape comparators in a continuing life. No instance of learning in the test proves spontaneous concern or meaning.

## Falsification
The learned predictor may merely memorize a motor-inertia response; the learned variant could lose to cheap persistence or motor means; RGB feature could be irrelevant; dark backstop may be identifiable only after touch; more examples could make a noisier model; same frame may still alias physically different outcomes; host-controlled motor provenance is a harness fact, not resident-authored decision.

**Stop:** Report positive/negative/underpowered transparently after native CI. Do not tune thresholds or scenes to rescue a family. If cheap alternatives are sufficient, shift research effort to how a continuing actor acquires *relevant ongoing concerns* and later investigates an ambiguity, not a larger predictor. Keep this a donor experiment, not a new canonical 'P0 brain'.
