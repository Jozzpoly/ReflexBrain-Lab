# RB-LIFE/L0 — ordinary-life causal pressure survey (2026-10-21)
**PRE-EXECUTION FROZEN. Diagnostic, NOT a learned-organism or H7/H8/H9 qualification.**
Live substrate exact PR #81 source (B0/B1, DevRuntime, DevResident), unchanged behavior and parameters. Research-only branch; no main/Pages merge or new actor code.

## Motivation
The new ACT/P0 action→private-effect predictor on PR #82 was not reliable under material contact and was deliberately not promoted to autonomous epistemic control. The *stronger* missing issue: O-DEV/V0 has a tiny authored four-maneuver activity source and movement-valued score, but is there recurrent physically grounded **material pressure during ordinary life**, when no investigator prescribes a particular push? If not, adding curiosity would optimize noise/motor loops rather than meaningful lived alternatives.

## Frozen conditions
Real 120Hz Rapier, ordinary 30Hz retinal/touch/proprio loop and resident automatic decisions. No manual test oracle and no on-the-fly scene tuning. **Four conditions:** B1 adaptive yard, B1 frozen yard, B1 adaptive shifted, B1 frozen shifted. **Also two B0 adaptive morphology controls**, both layouts; same 120 simulated seconds each. This is a descriptive census, not randomized population statistics. World is unchanged and contains independent rail mover and several ordinary loose objects.

Track in four 30s quarters:
- resident path, distinct physical 4m spatial cells;
- actor-private touch-window count and fraction of sampled moments in slow forward stall while autonomously requesting positive forward drive;
- **host-only** contact samples partitioned by stationary walls, loose bodies and independent mover (do not turn these into actor labels);
- researcher-measured changes to actual loose bodies, with rail motion excluded;
- number of windows containing any visible nonbackground 96RGB rays; this does not prove recognition or causation;
- learned action-utility state and the chosen-action distribution.

No success threshold. Log exact source marker, test/CI SHA, negatives and uncertainty. These conditions assess the **opportunities and failure patterns of a primitive host**, not whether new semantics spontaneously occurred. Positive matter motion, vision or contacts are not H7 or 'curiosity'. A zero material interaction proves only that this inhabitant/layout did not engage during this span.

## Decision rule, frozen
If ordinary-life physical contact is absent, rare or stuck, return to H6 and revise basic activity/physical opportunity before learning a new significance organ. If recurring physical consequence exists but existing sensor/behavioral history has no continuing consumer, next work should be **one replaceable bounded ongoing activity that uses material information**, tested against cheap authored controls. Only once an actor-private event changes its continuing activity, and H7/H8/H9 recur across geometries, may a learned semantic mechanism be qualified. Do NOT use ACT/P0's prediction loss or O-DEV's motor score as surrogate purpose.

Do not extend this survey to months of tuning / bigger worlds. One source-run and honest conclusion.
