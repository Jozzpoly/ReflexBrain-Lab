# S0 Playmat — Owner-observed organism/world FAIL (video reviewed, 2026-10-11)

**Authority:** **OWNER FAIL — experiential/product-level**, overrules any earlier inference of a valuable/playable/convincing organism from green CI. The Owner personally tested and recorded the **publicly released Material Playmat**, then reported that the actor blindly follows a ball, gets stuck against walls, and acts in an unreasonably small cage. In the Owner's assessment a five-minute simple ball follower would have produced roughly the same apparent result; after days of research this is not a meaningful agent/intelligence experience.

## Observable grounds and epistemic limitations

- Owner-provided **~67.6-second video of actual public Playmat** was inspected directly by Browser ChatGPT (do **not** upload or publish the private original recording without explicit Owner permission). Representative frames at ~5, ~25 and ~40 seconds of **recording time** show repeated local crowding of the cyan movable body and blue actor, including along the lower-right World boundary. These seconds are video timestamps, **not** simulation timestamps: the Owner selected playback speeds and controller variants.
- At ~5s of recording, the host-only coarse target↔dock **gap display reads 0.00m**, while later scenes show positive gaps and repetitive physical contact. Zero is a clamped approximate geometry proxy and **cannot be promoted to sustained success** or a causal understanding of docking.
- At ~25 and ~40s of video, the main actor/target are at the **right/down boundary** in an apparent pushing/chasing loop. The displayed private state includes `seenDockAge:null` in some views, so it does not substantiate ongoing dock-specific awareness; the physical picture alone does not prove the precise neural/control failure path.
- Source confirms default yard consists of fixed walls at +/-10 x and +/-6 y (about **20×12 world units**), a comparatively large actor disc (radius ~1), sparse colored objects and one internal column; RGB color patches, directional touch and authored controllers are real but provide weak material/semantic possibilities. There is also an **amber lookalike** object distinguishable from the dock by the host, not reliably by the agent's color filter.
- Prior frozen S0 scientific material metric remains **negative** and unchanged after UI polish: after 60s, relational controller 2.90m proxy gap, simple cyan chaser 1.34m, independent World 2.43m. Machine evidence: physical influence and browser functionality **PASS**; maintained actor-owned relation, situated competence and Owner watchability **FAIL / NOT QUALIFIED**.
- Do not assume Owner intended a larger rectangular prison as the solution. Size alone does not create ecology, meaningful choices or an actor's own open-ended continuing activity.

## Diagnosis — separate the failures instead of another control tweak

**WORLD / RESEARCH-HOST MISMATCH:** The tiny walled box forces frequent wall encounters and very short repetitive trajectories. The environment provides too few independent, consequential processes, traversable relationships, affordances and opportunities for discovery or endogenous continuity. Even a much better brain is unlikely to express interesting systemic life there.

**ACTOR / AGENCY GAP:** The `MaterialConcern` remains a manually authored pursuit of visual color-target and dock proxy, not a learned/developmental local intellect. It does not robustly know when to stop pushing, when the relation is lost, when to seek information, or what else to do if pursuit is impossible. Lack of bounded/visible policy competence compounds the world's limitations.

**EVALUATION CATEGORY ERROR:** Instrumented microtest + 115/115 CI + attractive viewer did not deliver Owner-experienced intelligence. Owner's lived observation is the proper product-level gate. The Viewer is a technically usable microscope **and a failed organism/world specimen**; do not write "ready organism" or use responsive UI as organism achievement.

## Consequent project decision (not a frozen replacement architecture)

1. **S0 ceases to be the active organism product trajectory.** Preserve it as a negative physical/evaluation donor and minimal Viewer tooling; don't tune cyan bearing, stop force or fake success in the same yard; don't add a 100× map-only scaling patch to disguise the same activity.
2. **Recover the actual Owner vision and best existing donors**, including Persistent Playground's stronger observed watchability, O-CTRL's grounded causal world, autonomous L0/ecology work and other relevant projects. Re-evaluate which donors genuinely supply living World behavior instead of impressive dashboards. Prevent accidental subsystem takeover from Combat/SPC/Feniks.
3. **World/organism co-design is the next priority.** Research a larger *structured*, physically meaningful continuous space (not necessarily big/expensive): navigable regions and passages, areas of different physical affordances, movable matter, independently changing processes, bodily scale/motion that fits the geometry, and meaningful consequences of action/inaction. More room is necessary but insufficient; physically consistent causation is the objective.
4. **Demand early Owner eyes-on evidence.** A tiny playable vertical slice of this next World should be viewable and critiqued *before* a new complex model/curriculum/reward system is built. Cheap reactive/reflex controls are honest minimum baselines. Require a qualitatively different actor behavior than just cyan pursuit/collision, plus explicit difference in retained private evidence and future actions. Design scientifically valid falsifiers only after the subject and meaningful affordance actually exist.
5. **Do not choose final engine, representation, World topology or control stack from this note.** First inspect real donor/source code, screen behavior and Owner reactions. Choose a bounded next prototype that can be disproved cheaply; keep freedom to throw it away.
