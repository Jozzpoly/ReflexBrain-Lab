# 1. What is strongly recovered

Persistent Playground v1 had, at minimum:
- one persistent material playmat;
- one actor;
- manipulable material objects;
- **Owner takeover of the actor/body**;
- return to autonomous operation;
- separate World vs brain reset;
- prediction/debug lens;
- learning-progress exploration.

The strongest later synthesis explicitly says that the emerging "life feel" was attributed primarily to:

> embodied continuity + material consequences + bounded perception + ongoing exploration

rather than to the learner itself.

This is consistent with the broader recovered Owner signal:

> value comes from being able to stand beside something that has its own trajectory.

Therefore the likely value of takeover was not merely:
- "I can control the character".

It was the transition:

> **autonomous trajectory -> external authority enters -> lived physical consequences accumulate -> external authority releases -> autonomous aftermath continues.**

That transition is a high-value medium/research donor.

# 2. What is NOT recovered well enough

The exact historical takeover implementation is not currently recoverable from the available sources.

In particular, the current evidence does **not** establish:
- exact key-repeat handling;
- which inputs were repeated or dropped;
- whether learner updates happened every repeated input, only on change, or on experience windows;
- exact timing of Owner authority acquisition/release;
- whether actor-private motor-intention state was overwritten during takeover;
- whether takeover motion was labeled internally as self-caused;
- exact condition that produced the remembered "zero learning" bug;
- exact fix, if any;
- whether private history continued, paused, or was partially updated during takeover.

The donor audit preserves only higher-level warnings:
- takeover could produce misleading learning evidence;
- external manipulation could be misattributed as self-caused;
- old debug/experience segmentation was not trustworthy enough to canonize.

Therefore:

> **do not recreate the old takeover architecture.**

The old surface is a phenomenon donor, not a source-code contract.

# 3. Takeover is actually a self/world causality experiment

A new takeover should be framed as:

> What should an embodied actor privately experience when motor authority is temporarily external, if the actor is not given a semantic "Owner is controlling me" oracle?

This is substantially more interesting than a direct-control feature.

Possible causal layers must remain separate:

## World / body truth
- body actually moves;
- contacts happen;
- sensors change;
- objects are displaced;
- material consequences persist.

## motor command provenance
Research host knows:
- command came from Owner or local controller.

But this provenance is **not automatically actor knowledge**.

## actor-private motor evidence
The actor may have access to some combination of:
- intended/local motor demand;
- actual body motion;
- proprioceptive consequence;
- contact/tactile consequence;
- sensory changes.

Exactly which of these exist is an organism/sensorium decision.

## actor-private interpretation
Future learned/self-world systems may infer:
- "this change followed my own command";
- "my body moved without the expected command";
- "something external acted on me".

That inference must be earned.

# 4. Three takeover modes that must not be conflated

These are research candidates, not implementation choices.

## A. motor-authority substitution

Owner commands the same legal actuator seam that the local controller normally drives.

Characteristics:
- body remains material;
- actor sensors continue;
- local controller motor output is suppressed or bypassed;
- research provenance records Owner authority.

This is the closest candidate to a clean takeover experiment.

Open question:
does actor-private state retain its own suppressed motor intention?

## B. physical external manipulation

Owner applies force/constraint to the body as a World intervention.

Characteristics:
- local motor controller may continue issuing commands;
- body motion differs because of external physical cause;
- potentially strongest reafference/exafference pressure.

This is **not** the same as takeover.

## C. teleport / authoritative pose manipulation

Owner directly sets pose/velocity.

Useful for:
- setup;
- pathological-state recovery;
- researcher interventions.

But it is the least ordinary physical cause and should be visibly marked as such.

Do not call C "takeover".

---

# 5. RELEASE is the scientifically interesting boundary

Historical medium design treated takeover mainly as a mode.

The stronger research framing is:

> **release creates a causal epoch boundary.**

At release we can ask:
- what private state survived?
- what material consequences survived?
- does ordinary behavior resume as if nothing happened?
- does the actor compensate for displacement?
- does it revisit/check changed surroundings?
- does private history later alter behavior?
- did the actor incorrectly attribute Owner-caused motion to itself?

Future medium should make RELEASE bookmarkable in provenance.

It should not automatically tell the actor:
- "Owner released control";
- "this was not your action".

# 6. Candidate experiment families — not activated

## TAKE-R1 — authority substitution null

Frozen simple controller/body.

Compare:
- ordinary autonomous motor commands;
- Owner substitutes commands through same actuator seam for a bounded interval;
- then release.

Measure:
- World/body trajectory;
- private proprioception;
- local motor-intention trace;
- post-release continuation.

No learning needed.

Primary question:
does the host preserve clean causal provenance without leaking it to actor-private channels?

## TAKE-R2 — reafference/exafference pressure

Only after lawful motor-intention + body-consequence channels exist.

Matched body motion generated by:
- actor's own motor demand;
- external physical force / authority intervention.

Ask:
can a future local learned system distinguish causal histories from private temporal evidence?

This is a ReflexBrain-relevant pressure.

## TAKE-R3 — history after release

Only after private-history continuation is qualified.

Matched current state at release, different legitimate takeover histories.

Ask:
does later ordinary behavior diverge because lived private history differs?

This resembles G3/private-history causality, but with authority perturbation.
