# Pre-O0 Candidate Organism Families — 2026-10-06

Status: **F2 PAPER PROTOTYPES · COMPETING FAMILIES · NO IMPLEMENTATION**

Parent:
`PRE_O0_FOUNDATIONS_CAMPAIGN_2026-10-06.md`

Goal:

Construct qualitatively different minimal organism/world couplings before choosing a substrate.

All families must obey:
- Owner is not heartbeat;
- no hidden World/debug truth in actor policy;
- body/world consequences are material;
- actor-private history may differ from World truth;
- instrumentation remains research-only;
- no learned semantic ReflexBrain yet.

The families intentionally disagree on normativity, perception, memory and control.

---

# Family A — Grounded Maintainer

## Core idea

One actor has one authored but world-grounded continuing concern.

Example:
- keep a material condition satisfied at some encountered site.

World processes recurrently disturb the condition.

## Source of trajectory

External/authored concern + private evidence about whether/how it can currently be satisfied.

## Normativity

Weakly endogenous.

The actor cares because the concern is authored into it.

World state still matters materially, but "why this matters" comes from designer specification.

## Perception

Can tolerate relatively structured percepts.

Still must preserve:
- occlusion;
- stale knowledge;
- checked absence;
- no hidden live locations.

## Memory

Object/landmark relational memory works naturally.

## Strengths

- easiest to falsify;
- clear persistent pressure;
- supports stale memory, obstruction, recovery;
- easy to compare across layouts;
- good baseline organism;
- creates later semantic ambiguity when multiple concerns eventually coexist.

## Main deception risk

A well-built task bot may look organism-like.

The policy can become:
`if unsatisfied -> find source -> carry -> site`.

Even private memory and physics do not automatically create endogenous agency.

## Best role

**Baseline/control family**, not assumed final organism.

# Family B — Adaptive Homeostat

## Core idea

The actor has one or several internal body variables that drift through world coupling.

Examples:
- energy availability;
- temperature;
- body integrity;
- local charge;
- actuator capacity.

World interaction can restore or worsen them.

No explicit "do maintenance task" concern.

## Source of trajectory

Maintaining viable operating range while continuing to interact.

## Normativity

More endogenous:
some world states alter the actor's own future functional capacity.

## Perception

Can begin relatively low-level because body/interoceptive change itself supplies significance.

## Memory

Needs memory mainly when delayed consequences/resource locations matter.

## Strengths

- world events matter relative to the actor;
- naturally actor-specific;
- gives real stakes;
- can make same object/environment state mean different things at different body states;
- potential bridge to affordance/body-scaled meaning.

## Main deception risks

### safe-idle attractor
Best policy may be "do almost nothing".

### fake metabolism
Designer picks arbitrary scalar and calls it life.

### reward leakage
Homeostatic error becomes universal objective/reward and central planner.

### trivial foraging bot
"energy low -> go charger" is still a task machine.

## Necessary falsifier

The organism must exhibit meaningful behavior beyond direct threshold reactions.

Body state should modulate existing competences/choices rather than fully determine them.

## Best role

Strong candidate for **normativity donor**, not necessarily whole architecture.

# Family C — Sensorimotor Play / Developmental Explorer

## Core idea

The actor has no fixed external job.

It explores a rich field of objects/processes with different lawful action-consequence structures.

The system develops competence through sensorimotor contingencies.

Possible intrinsic pressure:
- learning progress;
- competence improvement;
- controllability gain;
- self-generated local goals.

## Source of trajectory

Self-directed exploration of learnable action-effect structure.

## Normativity

Developmental:
situations matter because they change what the organism can predict/control/do.

## Perception

Should begin below semantic object labels if possible.

Tracklets / local physical signatures are preferable.

## Memory

Sensorimotor episodes, contingency models, skill/outcome memory.

## Strengths

- closest to old Persistent Playground's positive direction but can be redesigned;
- naturally produces body/world learning;
- compatible with reafference;
- can develop affordance-like competence without labels;
- potentially creates genuinely novel later ReflexBrain pressure.

## Main deception risks

- wall-clinging;
- noisy-TV traps;
- representation artifacts;
- endless play with no persistent identity;
- surprise/learning metric becomes hidden reward;
- life feel from random motor punctuation.

## Necessary falsifiers

Include:
- unlearnable noisy process;
- easy but useless controllable process;
- repeated familiar object;
- new object with same contingency;
- same object with altered contingency;
- Owner perturbation.

The actor should not merely chase maximal error/variance.

## Best role

Strong candidate for **developmental competence**, possibly paired later with another source of normativity.

# Family D — Habit / Sensorimotor Identity Organism

## Core idea

Persistent patterns of perception-action become self-maintaining habits.

The organism is organized around several sensorimotor identities rather than explicit goals.

Examples:
- patrol/explore rhythm;
- object interaction routine;
- return/reorient cycle;
- alternating local activity patterns.

Perturbations can destabilize a habit and force reorganization.

## Source of trajectory

Ongoing dynamical organization itself.

## Normativity

A perturbation matters because it disrupts or supports currently self-maintaining sensorimotor organization.

## Perception

Can be relatively low-level.

## Memory

May be distributed in recurrent/dynamical state rather than explicit symbolic beliefs.

## Strengths

- potentially produces continuity without task phases;
- aligns with enactive/habit notions;
- could make meaning emerge from disruption of ongoing organization;
- may avoid a central planner.

## Main deception risks

Project has already seen:
- deterministic limit cycles;
- complex switching mistaken for life;
- sticky attractors;
- observer anthropomorphism.

## Necessary falsifiers

- perturb body/environment parameters;
- change scene topology;
- run from many initial states;
- test for short hidden cycles;
- compare open-loop sensory replay;
- require adaptive post-perturbation reorganization rather than return to fixed attractor.

## Best role

High-risk/high-value research family.

Do not trust visual complexity.

# Family E — Relational Dyad

## Core idea

Two simple autonomous embodied actors share one material ecology.

Neither exists for the Owner.

Each has private perception/history and an independent ongoing trajectory.

Their paths occasionally intersect.

They need not begin with rich social cognition.

Possible relations:
- shared scarce object;
- complementary material process;
- physical obstruction;
- accidental following;
- exchange;
- local communication later.

## Source of trajectory

Each actor's own ordinary local organization plus independent actions of the other.

## Normativity

Mixed:
the same event can become actor-relative because each actor has a different body/history/current activity.

## Perception

Private and occluded.

Identity of the other actor may begin as perceptual continuity rather than semantic role.

## Memory

Contact/encounter history becomes useful naturally.

## Strengths

- produces independent causal novelty without Owner;
- directly creates same-event/different-actor meaning;
- world can keep happening elsewhere;
- closer to Mira/Janek intuition;
- strong future SPC donor.

## Main deception risks

- anthropomorphism: two moving dots look social;
- combinatorial complexity;
- hard causal diagnosis;
- each actor can still be a trivial task bot;
- social interaction can hide weak embodiment.

## Necessary falsifiers

- run each actor alone;
- run both with interaction disabled;
- swap private histories;
- identical body/world but different encounter history;
- perturb one actor while observing whether effects propagate through legal channels.

## Best role

Potential **ecology amplifier** after one-actor substrate is understood, or earlier if one-actor worlds remain causally poor.

# Family F — Independent Dynamic World + One Actor

## Core idea

One cognitive actor, but the environment contains several autonomous non-agent processes.

Examples:
- moving physical object/process;
- periodic/aperiodic door or passage changes;
- conveyor/water/field-like transport;
- resource growth/decay;
- object transformations;
- light/sound source moving independently.

## Source of trajectory

Actor's own competence interacts with world processes that continue without it.

## Normativity

Depends on what concern/organization accompanies the actor.

This family deliberately separates:
"independent causal world" from "second mind".

## Perception

Excellent test bed for reafference/exafference and active perception.

## Memory

Useful because world can change while unobserved.

## Strengths

- produces exogenous events without social complexity;
- cleaner causal attribution than dyad;
- makes stale memory naturally relevant;
- can test whether second actor is truly necessary.

## Main deception risks

- environment becomes a scripted theme-park;
- periodic processes become easy timing codes;
- actor learns scheduler phase rather than world relations.

## Necessary falsifiers

- randomized phase/period;
- non-periodic but lawful process;
- swap process parameters;
- remove one process;
- hidden changes during absence.

## Best role

Very strong **intermediate ecology family**.

# Family G — Minimal Hybrid Autonomous Organism

## Core idea

Combine only two independently qualified sources:

1. small endogenous body/habit/viability pressure;
2. developmental sensorimotor exploration or one world-grounded concern.

Not allowed until donor mechanisms are separately understood.

## Why it is tempting

Pure homeostat can be inert.
Pure explorer can lack stakes.
Pure maintainer can be task-bot.
Pure habit can be limit cycle.

A minimal hybrid could create:
- persistent ordinary concern;
- spare capacity for exploration;
- tradeoffs;
- actor-relative semantic pressure.

## Main deception risk

Hybridization can hide failure.

If the result looks good, attribution becomes unclear.

## Promotion rule

Do not build Family G until at least two component families each produce qualified distinct value.

# Shared adversarial walkthroughs

Every candidate family must answer these on paper before any code.

## W0 — Owner disappears

What happens for 5 minutes?

Reject answers equivalent to:
- fixed short loop;
- random wandering;
- inactive safe attractor unless inactivity is contextually earned.

## W1 — hidden world change

Something relevant changes outside perception.

What does actor believe?
What makes it check?
How does stale knowledge die?

## W2 — matched self/external sensory change

Produce nearly the same local sensory displacement through:
- actor action;
- external world/Owner perturbation.

What internal evidence differs?

## W3 — blocked current activity

Physical continuation stops making progress.

What keeps it from:
- thrashing;
- pushing forever;
- magically switching to hidden route?

## W4 — irrelevant perturbation

Strong but irrelevant physical change.

What prevents global adaptation/relearning?

## W5 — scene rearrangement

Same mechanics, materially new geometry.

What transfers?
What must be relearned?

## W6 — body dynamics changed

Mass/traction/turn dynamics change.

Does competence adapt?
Does perception of possibility become stale?

## W7 — history pair

Current local observation is approximately matched.
Relevant private history differs.

Can behavior legitimately diverge?

## W8 — unlearnable/noisy process

Does the organism become obsessed with unpredictability?

## W9 — Owner watches only

Is there anything worth observing without interaction?

# F2 current judgement

Do not pick one winner yet.

The most informative comparison appears to be:

1. **A — grounded maintainer** as a transparent task-bot baseline;
2. **C — sensorimotor developmental explorer** as a non-task developmental baseline;
3. **F — one actor in independently changing world** as a causal-ecology amplifier;
4. **B or D** as two different attempts at endogenous normativity;
5. **E — dyad** held as a strategic pressure if single-actor families remain impoverished.

The important new possibility is that **F may be more foundational than A**:

A richer independently changing world might be needed before deciding what the actor's concern should be.

Next phase:
walk these families through W0-W9 in more detail and identify which questions can be answered without implementation.
