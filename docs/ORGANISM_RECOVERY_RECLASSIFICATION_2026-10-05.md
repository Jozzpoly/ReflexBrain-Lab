## Recovery vertical slice: O0

Working name: **O0 — Persistent Organism Recovery**

The smallest useful O0 should contain one actor and one small persistent world.

Required properties:

1. **Zero-input persistence**  
   With no Owner input, the actor/world continue to produce real state change.

2. **One endogenous ongoing concern**  
   The actor has at least one continuing, world-grounded concern/activity. It must not be advanced by Owner clicks.

3. **Material continuation**  
   The actor attempts a continuation through actual motor demand/body/world dynamics. A continuation id alone is not evidence.

4. **Obstruction and recovery**  
   The world can make the current continuation fail or lose progress. The actor can locally recover, redirect or abandon based on legal evidence.

5. **Private experience**  
   Behavioural state may only consume information available to the actor. Debug/oracle truth remains researcher-only.

6. **Persistent history**  
   A previous material outcome can change a later local response without a global authored phase switch.

7. **Owner perturbation**  
   The Owner may enter, shove, obstruct, move an object or temporarily take over, but removing Owner input must not stop the organism.

8. **Microscope parity**  
   Preserve E0 replay/counterfactual/contact instrumentation around the richer organism.

## Explicit non-goals for O0

Do not require yet:
- learned ReflexBrain;
- LLM/SPC;
- complex social behaviour;
- crowd simulation;
- full viability/homeostasis;
- fatigue/depletion unless pressure earns it;
- universal planner;
- global reward;
- rich final art/world.

The goal is not to make a feature-complete NPC. The goal is to recover **an actually observable organism loop** before returning to learned meaning.

## Falsifiers

O0 is a FAIL if:
- the actor becomes inert without Owner input;
- it only performs a short fixed animation/route and resets;
- continuation is merely a timer, phase enum or target id with framewise steering underneath;
- the Owner must manufacture every meaningful event;
- debug/oracle state drives behaviour;
- the system is interesting only because the microscope is complicated;
- replay correctness is mistaken for organism value.

A simple deterministic Local Brain is allowed to pass O0 if the organism-level phenomenon is real. Learned intelligence is not the question yet.
