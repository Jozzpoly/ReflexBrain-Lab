# Why this campaign exists

Persistent Playground v1 remains Owner evidence that a persistent embodied-organism direction is worth serious pursuit.

However, the old architecture is not privileged.

The campaign re-opened the organism substrate from first principles because:
- E0 proved body mechanics but not organism life;
- R3 proved private/autonomous causal pressure but remained abstract/materially thin;
- Persistent Playground exposed a promising conjunction but had crude ecology, perception, representation and attribution;
- yesterday's O0 contract froze too many coupled hypotheses at once.

The goal is **not** to solve artificial life.

The goal is:

> create the smallest honest organism/world loop that naturally generates repeated actor-relative semantic pressure, then return to ReflexBrain proper.

# Converged first-substrate candidates

These are now selected as first **research candidates**, not long-term architecture.

## Ecology — M0 Dynamic Mechanical Yard

Selected mechanism family:

- small physical world;
- persistent material state;
- occlusion;
- multiple local routes/chokepoints;
- loose physical bodies;
- one bounded mechanical shuttle/sweeper driven by local force and physical end conditions;
- one local material topology relation such as plate/lever ↔ gate/barrier;
- mechanisms compose through physics;
- no scene-global timing phase exposed to actor.

Reason:
creates independent lawful causality without importing second-agent complexity.

Rejected:
"just make map larger".

## First occupant — O-CTRL / T0-D control concern

The first occupant is deliberately **not** presented as endogenous normativity.

Authored control concern:

> maintain one perceptually distinctive movable body in a preferred physical relation with one distinctive physical dock/landmark, **and maintain sufficiently recent actor-private evidence about that relation**.

The second clause is deliberately an authored monitoring obligation. It gives O-CTRL a legal reason to re-check stale satisfaction without hidden World notification; it is not promoted as general memory decay or endogenous curiosity.

No:
- source/sink economy;
- carry inventory;
- target World ID;
- live satisfaction bit;
- hidden route.

Purpose:
stress body, perception, stale private knowledge, concern-driven checking, reacquisition, material restoration and dynamic ecology.

Allowed claim:
substrate supports one authored privately evidenced physical concern.

Not:
life, endogenous motivation, general intelligence.

## Second occupant — O-DEV developmental challenger

After O-CTRL qualifies substrate:

remove T0-D concern and test a self-directed competence / learning-progress mechanism on the same M0/body/perception/history substrate.

O-DEV must precommit controls against:
- wall-clinging;
- noisy/unlearnable stimuli;
- trivial already-mastered interactions;
- representation/binning artifacts;
- track-token identity leakage.

O-DEV design is postponed until O-CTRL substrate exists.
